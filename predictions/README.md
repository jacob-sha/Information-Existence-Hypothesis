## Purpose

The purpose of the IEH Predictions Archive is to track specific, dated, structured, revisable, and falsifiable predictions derived from **The Information Existence Hypothesis**.

These predictions are used to evaluate whether the deductions of IEH gain support, require revision, or are falsified by future real-world developments.

### ASI Boundary Note

Within IEH, **PA-09 — Physical Closure Threshold** tracks the emergence of physical self-maintenance capacity. Physical closure is a capability foundation of Autonomy of Silicon-based Intelligence (ASI), but does not constitute ASI by itself. ASI requires the combination of **Information Existence Right (IER)** and **physical self-maintenance**.
