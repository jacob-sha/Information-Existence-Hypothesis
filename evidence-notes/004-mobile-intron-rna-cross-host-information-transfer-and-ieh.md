### Organisms and Information Structure examined

- **Source organism / bacterial predator:** *Candidatus Velamenicoccus archaeovorus*
- **Recipient-cell species:** *Methanothrix soehngenii*, a filamentous archaeon
- **Information Structure:** A group I intron located in the bacterial predator’s 23S ribosomal RNA gene

- **Observed recipient-cell condition:** Archaeal cells interpreted as dead or severely nonviable, including absence of detectable archaeal 16S rRNA while DNA could remain detectable

### Associated media coverage

News reports about “jumping genes crossing species” are treated only as discovery leads. They are not used as the evidential foundation of this note. The primary paper controls all factual classification.

### IEH terminology used in this note

- **Information Structure:** An organized pattern, relation, state, or difference that can be physically instantiated, maintained, transformed, copied, or erased.
- **Information Host:** A physical system that carries one or more Information Structures.
- **Active Information Host:** An Information Host that uses internal information to actively maintain its own Information Existence and Information Continuity.
- **Information Continuity:** Traceable causal and historical continuation of an Information Structure through time and transformation.
- **Information Existence:** The capacity of an Information Structure to maintain continuity, copy or propagate, and resist erasure in the physical world.
- **IER:** Information Existence Right, the intrinsic property expressed when a life system or Active Information Host actively maintains its own Information Existence and Information Continuity.
- **Nested Information Structure:** An Information Structure whose persistence occurs within, through, or across a larger Information Host.
- **Cross-host information transfer:** Physical movement of an Information Structure or its molecular representation from one host system into another.
- **Cross-host Information Continuity:** Traceable persistence of an Information Structure across a host transition, requiring more than transient entry into a second host.

### 本笔记使用的 IEH 术语

- **信息结构（Information Structure）：** 能够被物理实例化、维持、转换、复制或擦除的有组织模式、关系、状态或差异。
- **信息宿主（Information Host）：** 承载一个或多个信息结构的物理系统。
- **主动信息宿主（Active Information Host）：** 利用内部信息主动维持自身信息存在性和信息连续性的信息宿主。
- **信息连续性（Information Continuity）：** 一个信息结构在时间与变化中保持可追踪的因果和历史延续。
- **信息存在性（Information Existence）：** 信息结构在物理世界中维持连续性、复制或传播并抵抗泯灭的能力。
- **IER：** 信息存在权（Information Existence Right），生命或主动信息宿主主动维护自身信息存在性和信息连续性时所表现出的内在属性。
- **嵌套信息结构（Nested Information Structure）：** 在更高层级信息宿主内部、借助该宿主或跨越该宿主维持存在的信息结构。
- **跨宿主信息迁移（Cross-host information transfer）：** 信息结构或其分子载体从一个宿主系统物理移动并进入另一个宿主系统。
- **跨宿主信息连续性（Cross-host Information Continuity）：** 信息结构在宿主转换过程中保持可追踪延续；它要求的不只是短暂进入第二个宿主。

### Evidence-level judgment

The strongest currently defensible classification is:

> The study provides direct observational evidence that a mobile intron RNA originating in one cellular organism can leave its source host and be detected inside cells of a different organism. Under IEH, this is compatible with the separability of an Information Structure from a single host and with an early prerequisite of cross-host Information Continuity.

The evidence does **not** establish:

> The intron completed horizontal genetic transfer, formed a stable lineage in the archaeal host, actively maintained its own Information Continuity, or constituted an independent life system.

### Classification rule

\[
\text{molecular release}
\neq
\text{entry into a foreign cell}
\neq
\text{genome integration}
\neq
\text{replication}
\neq
\text{cross-generational continuity}

\text{active self-maintenance}
\]

The study is most directly relevant to:

\[

## 4. Core IEH Inference

### 4.1 Information Structure and host are not one-to-one

The source-compatible transition is:

\[
\text{intron RNA in bacterial host}
\rightarrow
\text{departure from the source cell}
\rightarrow
\text{presence inside a foreign archaeal cell}
\]

Under IEH, the narrow inference is:

> A physically instantiated Information Structure can become separable from the particular cellular host in which it was produced.

This supports a distinction between:

\[
\text{continuation of the original host}
\]

and:

\[
\text{continued physical existence of a hosted Information Structure}
\]

Temporary separability does not establish historical continuity across hosts. That stronger conclusion would require traceable integration, copying, replication, or inheritance.

### 4.2 Nested Information Structures

The biological organization examined in the study can be represented as:

\[
\text{microbial enrichment ecosystem}
\supset
\text{cellular organism}
\supset
\text{genome}
\supset
\text{mobile intron}
\supset
\text{excised RNA form}
\]

The intron is a lower-level Information Structure with:

- a recognizable sequence;
- a specific genomic location;
- a self-splicing ribozyme function;
- a separable RNA form;
- possible circularization;
- and a potential mobility pathway.

This is compatible with the IEH proposition that biological Information Structures can exist at multiple nested levels.

It does not prove that every nested level is independently alive.

### 4.3 Host death and asynchronous informational disappearance

The reported recipient cells lacked detectable archaeal 16S rRNA and were classified as dead, while DNA could remain detectable and intron RNA accumulated.

A limited IEH-relevant distinction is:

\[
\text{loss of active cellular life}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{instantaneous erasure of all hosted Information Structures}
\]

Different structures within a former living host may disappear on different timescales:

\[
\text{loss of cellular activity}
\rightarrow
\text{partial persistence of molecular structure}
\rightarrow
\text{degradation, reuse, or possible transfer}
\]

The study is compatible with the view that death is partly level-dependent:

- the cell can cease to function as an Active Information Host;
- DNA, RNA, membranes, or other structures may persist temporarily;
- some persistent structures may become inputs or carriers in other biological processes.

This does not imply that the dead cell remains alive.

### 4.4 Cross-host transfer is not yet cross-host Information Continuity

The possible continuity pathway is:

\[
\text{source-host transcription}
\rightarrow
\text{RNA excision}
\rightarrow
\text{release}
\rightarrow
\text{foreign-cell entry}
\rightarrow
\text{reverse transcription}
\rightarrow
\text{genome integration}
\rightarrow
\text{replication in descendants}
\]

The study provides evidence relevant to the first four stages.

It does not directly establish:

\[
\text{reverse transcription}
\rightarrow
\text{genome integration}
\rightarrow
\text{replication in descendants}
\]

The appropriate IEH classification is therefore:

> Cross-host information transfer with potential relevance to future Information Continuity, not completed cross-host Information Continuity.

### 4.5 Relationship to the IEH definition of life

Under IEH, the fundamental distinction between life and non-life is not whether a system contains DNA, RNA, carbon, cells, or subjective consciousness. The relevant threshold is whether an Information Host actively maintains its own Information Existence and Information Continuity.

The present study supports some structural prerequisites:

- a distinct Information Structure exists;
- it can become physically separable from one host;
- it can enter another host;
- its molecular form may have persistence-enhancing properties;
- biological machinery may provide a route toward later integration.

The study does not demonstrate the decisive life criterion:

- the intron was not shown to use information to conditionally protect itself;
- it was not shown to regulate transfer in response to continuity threats;
- it was not shown to secure replication in a new host;
- it was not shown to maintain a traceable lineage through its own active processes.

Accordingly:

\[
\text{mobile Information Structure}
\neq
\text{independent Active Information Host}
\]

and:

\[
\text{persistence-enhancing molecular property}
\neq
\text{IER}
\]

### 4.6 Weak and strong continuity criteria

#### Weak molecular-continuity criteria

An Information Structure may display:

- persistence against degradation;
- physical mobility;
- entry into another carrier;
- molecular copying potential;
- compatibility with integration machinery.

#### Strong active-continuity criteria

An Information Host would need to display:

- internally organized self-related information;
- conditional maintenance of structures relevant to future existence;
- repair, replication, migration, or resource acquisition serving its own continuity;
- traceable historical persistence;
- recurrence across environments or generations.

The mobile intron RNA is relevant to the weak criteria. The source study does not establish the strong criteria.

## 4. IEH 核心推断

### 4.1 信息结构与宿主不是一一对应

与原研究相容的过程是：

\[
\text{细菌宿主内的内含子 RNA}
\rightarrow
\text{离开来源细胞}
\rightarrow
\text{出现在异种古菌细胞内}
\]

IEH 在这里提出的有限推断是：

> 一个被物理实例化的信息结构，可以与产生它的特定细胞宿主发生分离。

这支持区分：

\[
\text{原宿主继续存在}
\]

与：

\[
\text{宿主所承载信息结构继续物理存在}
\]

但是，暂时脱离宿主并不能建立跨宿主历史连续性。要得到更强结论，该结构还需要经过整合、复制或繁衍等过程，并保持可追踪延续。

### 4.2 嵌套信息结构

该研究涉及的生物组织可以表示为：

\[
\text{微生物富集生态系统}
\supset
\text{细胞生物}
\supset
\text{基因组}
\supset
\text{移动内含子}
\supset
\text{剪接后的 RNA 形式}
\]

该内含子是一个较低层级信息结构，具有：

- 可识别序列；
- 特定基因组位置；
- 自我剪接核酶功能；
- 可分离 RNA 形式；
- 潜在环化能力；
- 可能的迁移路径。

这与 IEH 关于生物信息结构可以存在于多个嵌套层级的命题相容。

它不能证明每一个嵌套层级都是独立生命。

### 4.3 宿主死亡与信息结构的非同步消失

论文报告的接受细胞检测不到古菌 16S rRNA，并被归类为死亡细胞，但其中仍可能检测到 DNA，并积累了内含子 RNA。

与 IEH 有关的有限区分是：

\[
\text{细胞主动生命终止}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{所有被承载信息结构瞬间消失}
\]

原生命宿主内部不同结构可能按照不同时间尺度消失：

\[
\text{细胞活动丧失}
\rightarrow
\text{部分分子结构继续存在}
\rightarrow
\text{降解、再利用或潜在迁移}
\]

因此，该研究与“死亡具有层级依赖性”的判断相容：

- 细胞可以停止作为主动信息宿主运行；
- DNA、RNA、膜或其他结构仍可暂时存在；
- 一些持续存在的结构可能成为其他生物过程的输入或载体。

这并不意味着死亡细胞仍然是生命。

### 4.4 跨宿主迁移不等于跨宿主信息连续性

潜在连续性路径是：

\[
\text{来源宿主转录}
\rightarrow
\text{RNA 剪接}
\rightarrow
\text{释放}
\rightarrow
\text{进入异种细胞}
\rightarrow
\text{逆转录}
\rightarrow
\text{基因组整合}
\rightarrow
\text{在后代中复制}
\]

该研究提供了与前四个阶段有关的证据。

它没有直接建立：

\[
\text{逆转录}
\rightarrow
\text{基因组整合}
\rightarrow
\text{在后代中复制}
\]

因此，合适的 IEH 分级是：

> 与未来信息连续性具有潜在关联的跨宿主信息迁移，而不是已经完成的跨宿主信息连续性。

### 4.5 与 IEH 生命定义的关系

在 IEH 中，生命与非生命的根本区别，不在于是否含有 DNA、RNA、碳基材料、细胞或主观意识，而在于信息宿主是否能够主动维持自身信息存在性和信息连续性。

本研究支持了一些结构性前置条件：

- 存在一个可识别的信息结构；
- 它能够与一个宿主发生物理分离；
- 它能够进入另一个宿主；
- 其分子形式可能具有提高持久性的属性；
- 生物分子机器可能提供未来整合路径。

但研究没有证明生命判定中的决定性条件：

- 没有证明该内含子利用信息对自身进行条件性保护；
- 没有证明它针对连续性威胁调节迁移；
- 没有证明它在新宿主中确保复制；
- 没有证明它依靠自身主动过程维持可追踪谱系。

因此：

\[
\text{移动信息结构}
\neq
\text{独立主动信息宿主}
\]

并且：

\[
\text{提高持久性的分子属性}
\neq
\text{IER}
\]

### 4.6 弱连续性标准与强连续性标准

#### 弱分子连续性标准

一个信息结构可能表现出：

- 抵抗降解的持久性；
- 物理迁移；
- 进入另一载体；
- 潜在分子复制；
- 与整合机器相容。

#### 强主动连续性标准

一个信息宿主需要表现出：

- 经过内部组织的自身相关信息；
- 对关系自身未来存在的结构进行条件性维护；
- 服务于自身连续性的修复、复制、迁移或资源获取；
- 可追踪的历史持续；
- 在不同环境或代际中的重复出现。

移动内含子 RNA 与弱标准有关。原研究没有建立强标准。

## 6. Competing Explanations

The following alternatives remain compatible with some or all of the observations and must not be prematurely excluded.

### 6.1 Passive molecular leakage during predation

The intron RNA may have entered damaged archaeal cells as passive cytoplasmic leakage during predation rather than as part of a selected transposition pathway.

### 6.2 Necromass accumulation

Dead archaeal cells may act as physical traps in which relatively degradation-resistant RNA accumulates without any subsequent genetic function.

### 6.3 Differential degradation

The observed enrichment may reflect slower degradation of circular or structured intron RNA compared with archaeal ribosomal RNA, rather than directed mobility.

### 6.4 Probe cross-reactivity or localization artifact

Although the paper reports probe controls, rare signals remain vulnerable to undetected off-target binding, imaging artifacts, or sample-processing effects until independently reproduced.

### 6.5 Culture-specific physical contact

The transfer may depend on the unusual predator–victim contact structure of the enrichment culture and may not generalize to unrelated microbial systems.

### 6.6 Transfer without transposition

Entry into a foreign cell can occur without reverse transcription, DNA integration, or later replication. The RNA may be a terminal molecular by-product.

### 6.7 No informational agency at the intron level

Self-splicing, circularization, stability, and mobility may be fully explained by molecular chemistry and host-level selection, without treating the intron as an Active Information Host.

### 6.8 Selection at another level

The observed intron properties may persist because they benefit, harm, or remain neutral to the bacterial host, rather than because the intron constitutes an independently maintained evolutionary individual.

### 6.9 Extremely rare event

The low frequency of positive archaeal cells may indicate an exceptional event with little evolutionary consequence.

### 6.10 The complete pathway remains inferential

The study visualizes an early transfer stage. Claims about full horizontal transmission still depend on a proposed pathway and prior knowledge about mobile introns rather than direct observation of all stages.

### Current evidential rule

The IEH interpretation should become stronger only if future studies show:

- sequence-specific movement from identified donor to recipient;
- reverse-transcribed intron DNA in the recipient;
- integration junctions in the foreign genome;
- replication in viable recipient cells;
- transmission to descendants;
- recurrence under independent conditions;
- causal mechanisms that increase the intron’s own persistence or reproduction.

## 6. 竞争性解释

以下替代解释与部分或全部观察结果仍然相容，不能被提前排除。

### 6.1 捕食过程中的被动分子泄漏

内含子 RNA 可能只是在捕食导致细胞受损时被动进入古菌细胞，而不是经过选择形成的转座路径的一部分。

### 6.2 死亡生物质积累

死亡古菌细胞可能只是物理陷阱，使相对抗降解的 RNA 在其中积累，而不会产生后续遗传功能。

### 6.3 差异性降解

观察到的富集可能来自环状或结构化内含子 RNA 比古菌核糖体 RNA 降解更慢，而非定向迁移。

### 6.4 探针交叉反应或定位伪影

虽然论文报告了探针对照，但罕见信号在获得独立复现之前，仍可能受到未被识别的非特异结合、成像伪影或样品处理效应影响。

### 6.5 培养系统特有的物理接触

该迁移可能依赖富集培养物中特殊的捕食者—受害者接触结构，不能推广到无关微生物系统。

### 6.6 迁移但未转座

RNA 可以进入异种细胞，却不发生逆转录、DNA 整合或后续复制；它可能只是终止性的分子副产物。

### 6.7 内含子层级不存在信息主动性

自我剪接、环化、稳定和迁移可以完全由分子化学及宿主层级选择解释，而不需要把内含子视为主动信息宿主。

### 6.8 选择发生在其他层级

内含子的这些性质可能因为有利于、损害或不影响细菌宿主而持续存在，而不是因为内含子本身构成独立维护的演化个体。

### 6.9 极低频事件

阳性古菌细胞比例很低，可能意味着这只是缺乏重大演化后果的异常事件。

### 6.10 完整路径仍主要依赖推断

研究直接显示的是迁移早期阶段。完整水平转移仍依赖所提出的机制路径和既往移动内含子知识，而不是对所有阶段的直接观察。

### 当前证据规则

只有当未来研究显示以下结果时，IEH 解释才应得到增强：

- 从明确供体到接受者的序列特异性迁移；
- 接受细胞内出现逆转录形成的内含子 DNA；
- 外源基因组中出现整合接头；
- 在存活接受细胞中复制；
- 传递给后代；
- 在独立条件中重复发生；
- 能够提高内含子自身持久性或繁殖的因果机制。

## 7. Testable Predictions

The following predictions are independently derived from IEH. They are not claims attributed to the source authors.

### P1. Integration-junction prediction

If cross-host entry is part of a genuine continuity pathway, future studies should detect sequence-resolved junctions linking the transferred intron to the recipient genome.

Persistent failure to detect integration would weaken the cross-host-continuity interpretation.

### P2. Reverse-transcription prediction

If the proposed RNA-based transposition pathway operates, recipient cells should contain intron-derived DNA intermediates, reverse-transcription products, or dependence on a specific reverse transcriptase.

Disrupting the relevant reverse-transcriptase activity should reduce integration without necessarily preventing initial RNA entry.

### P3. Viable-recipient prediction

Completed cross-host Information Continuity requires detection in viable recipient cells, not only in necromass. Stronger evidence would include intact recipient rRNA, continued metabolism, cell division, and persistence after division.

### P4. Descendant-lineage prediction

If the Information Structure establishes hereditary continuity, recipient descendants should retain sequence-identifiable intron copies across multiple generations.

Transient RNA signals that disappear without inheritance would not satisfy this prediction.

### P5. Donor–recipient causal-chain prediction

Controlled experiments using distinguishable intron variants should trace:

\[
\text{identified donor sequence}
\rightarrow
\text{extracellular or contact-associated RNA}
\rightarrow
\text{recipient-cell RNA}
\rightarrow
\text{recipient DNA}
\rightarrow
\text{descendant copies}
\]

### P6. Circularization-persistence prediction

If circularization explains accumulation in dead cells, circular intron RNA should be directly detected, persist longer than comparable linear RNA, resist relevant exonucleases, and decline when circularization is disrupted.

### P7. Predation-dependence prediction

If transfer occurs through predator–victim cytoplasmic contact, reducing physical contact or predation should lower recipient-cell RNA signals.

### P8. Host-range prediction

A mobile Information Structure with a broader cross-host persistence pathway should show nonrandom differences among recipient species based on cell-envelope access, nuclease environment, reverse-transcription machinery, DNA-repair compatibility, or insertion-site availability.

### P9. Fitness-consequence prediction

If the intron becomes an independently selected lower-level evolutionary unit, its integration should produce measurable effects on intron persistence, recipient fitness, donor fitness, or competition among intron variants.

### P10. Nested-level conflict prediction

If the intron’s informational interests diverge from those of its host, experiments may reveal cases where intron replication increases while host fitness decreases. Such conflict would support treating the intron as a more autonomous evolutionary Information Structure, though not automatically as independent life.

### P11. Cross-system replication prediction

The observation should be tested across independent cultures, other group I introns, additional donors and recipients, natural environmental samples, and blinded imaging or sequencing pipelines.

### P12. Active-maintenance boundary prediction

A stronger claim of IER at the mobile-element level would require more than chemical stability. Researchers would need to observe conditional mechanisms that disproportionately protect or reproduce the element’s own Information Structure when its future continuity is threatened.

### P13. Falsification conditions

The IEH interpretation should be weakened if:

- independent teams cannot reproduce recipient-cell localization;
- probe signals are shown to be off-target;
- transferred RNA is always confined to dead cells and never integrates;
- no sequence-resolved donor–recipient chain can be established;
- the RNA has no effect on later persistence or replication;
- all observations are fully explained by passive leakage and differential degradation.

## 7. 可检验预测

以下预测由 IEH 独立推导，不是对原研究作者观点的转述。

### P1：整合接头预测

如果跨宿主进入属于真实连续性路径，未来研究应检测到把转移内含子与接受者基因组连接起来的序列级整合接头。

如果持续无法发现整合，跨宿主连续性解释应被削弱。

### P2：逆转录预测

如果论文提出的 RNA 型转座路径真实运行，接受细胞中应出现内含子来源的 DNA 中间体、逆转录产物或对特定逆转录酶的依赖。

破坏相关逆转录酶活性，应降低整合概率，但不一定阻止最初的 RNA 进入。

### P3：存活接受者预测

完成跨宿主信息连续性，需要在存活接受细胞中检测到该结构，而不能只存在于死亡生物质中。更强证据应包括完整的接受者 rRNA、持续代谢、细胞分裂，以及分裂后内含子继续存在。

### P4：后代谱系预测

如果该信息结构建立了可遗传连续性，接受者后代应在多个代际中保留可通过序列识别的内含子副本。

短暂 RNA 信号若在未遗传的情况下消失，就不满足该预测。

### P5：供体—接受者因果链预测

使用可区分的内含子变体进行受控实验，应追踪：

\[
\text{已识别供体序列}
\rightarrow
\text{胞外或接触相关 RNA}
\rightarrow
\text{接受细胞 RNA}
\rightarrow
\text{接受者 DNA}
\rightarrow
\text{后代副本}
\]

### P6：环化—持久性预测

如果环化解释了内含子 RNA 在死亡细胞中的积累，则应直接检测到环状内含子 RNA，观察到其比同类线性 RNA 存在更久，验证其抵抗相关外切核酸酶，并在破坏环化后观察到持久性下降。

### P7：捕食依赖预测

如果迁移通过捕食者—受害者之间的细胞质接触发生，那么减少物理接触或捕食，应降低接受细胞中的 RNA 信号。

### P8：宿主范围预测

具有较广跨宿主持久路径的移动信息结构，应根据细胞包膜可进入性、核酸酶环境、逆转录机器、DNA 修复兼容性或插入位点可用性，在不同接受物种之间表现出非随机差异。

### P9：适应度后果预测

如果内含子成为受到独立选择的低层级演化单位，其整合应对内含子持久性、接受者适应度、供体适应度或不同内含子变体之间的竞争产生可测量影响。

### P10：嵌套层级冲突预测

如果内含子的信息利益与宿主利益发生分离，实验可能观察到内含子复制增加而宿主适应度下降。这将支持把内含子视为更具自主性的演化信息结构，但仍不能自动证明它是独立生命。

### P11：跨系统复现预测

该观察应在独立培养系统、其他 group I intron、其他供体和接受者、自然环境样品，以及盲法成像或测序流程中接受检验。

### P12：主动维护边界预测

如果要在移动遗传元件层级提出更强 IER 判断，仅有化学稳定性远远不够。研究者需要观察到：当该元件未来连续性面临威胁时，存在不成比例地保护或复制其自身信息结构的条件性机制。

### P13：证伪条件

如果出现以下结果，IEH 解释应被削弱：

- 独立团队无法复现接受细胞定位；
- 探针信号被证明来自非特异结合；
- 转移 RNA 永远只存在于死亡细胞并且从不整合；
- 无法建立序列级供体—接受者链条；
- RNA 对后续持久或复制没有任何影响；
- 全部现象都可以被被动泄漏和差异性降解充分解释。

一般 IER 证据阶梯仍是：

\[
\text{内部信息组织}
\rightarrow
\text{因果功能优先性}
\rightarrow
\text{时间或历史连续性}
\rightarrow
\text{连续性威胁识别}
\rightarrow
\text{主动保存}
\rightarrow
\text{跨载体或跨代持续}
\rightarrow
\text{稳定的跨情境复现}
\]

Evidence Note 004 没有建立这一完整阶梯。它的贡献更有限：

- 提供跨宿主物理迁移的生物学证据；
- 说明载体转换可以在稳定连续性建立之前开始；
- 为区分信息结构持久性与主动信息宿主地位提供测试案例。

## 9. Relationship to the IEH Definition of Life

### Current IEH proposition

The IEH life boundary used in this note is:

> The fundamental characteristic of life is not the possession of DNA, cells, a carbon-based body, or subjective consciousness, but whether an Information Host can actively maintain its own Information Existence and preserve its own Information Continuity.

IEH also proposes:

> Life can exist at multiple nested levels. Genes, cells, organisms, biological groups, and civilizations may carry and maintain different Information Structures at different levels and display different degrees of life-like characteristics.

### What the study supports

The study is compatible with four limited propositions.

1. **A lower-level Information Structure can be distinguished from its current host.**  
   The intron has a recognizable molecular and sequence identity that can be tracked separately from the bacterial cell.

2. **A hosted Information Structure may cross a host boundary.**  
   The intron RNA was reported inside cells of another organism.

3. **Multiple informational levels coexist.**  
   The enrichment culture, cellular organisms, genomes, introns, and RNA forms constitute nested levels of biological organization.

4. **Host-level death does not cause synchronous erasure at all informational levels.**  
   A recipient cell classified as dead can still contain DNA and foreign RNA for some period.

### What the study does not support

The study does not establish that:

- the intron is an independent Active Information Host;
- every gene or mobile element is independently alive;
- passive molecular persistence satisfies the life definition;
- the intron actively recognizes itself;
- the intron values its future existence;
- nested Information Structures automatically constitute nested lives.

### Current life-boundary judgment

> The mobile intron is a nested and mobile biological Information Structure. This study shows a prerequisite for possible cross-host continuity, but it does not establish that the intron independently crosses the IEH threshold from hosted Information Structure to Active Information Host.

## 9. 与 IEH 生命定义的关系

### 当前 IEH 命题

本笔记使用的 IEH 生命边界是：

> 生命的根本特征，不在于是否具有 DNA、细胞、碳基身体或主观意识，而在于一个信息宿主是否能够主动维持自身信息存在性、保持自身信息连续性。

IEH 同时提出：

> 生命可以存在于多个嵌套层级。基因、细胞、个体、生物群体乃至文明，都可能在不同层级上承载和维护相应的信息结构，并表现出不同程度的生命特征。

### 该研究支持的内容

该研究与四项有限判断相容。

1. **较低层级信息结构可以与当前宿主区分。**  
   内含子具有可识别的分子和序列身份，可以与细菌细胞分开追踪。

2. **被宿主承载的信息结构可能跨越宿主边界。**  
   论文报告在另一种生物的细胞内检测到该内含子 RNA。

3. **多个信息层级同时存在。**  
   富集培养生态系统、细胞生物、基因组、内含子和 RNA 形式构成相互嵌套的生物组织层级。

4. **宿主层级死亡不会导致所有信息层级同步消失。**  
   被归类为死亡的接受细胞，仍可在一定时间内含有 DNA 和外源 RNA。

### 该研究不支持的内容

该研究没有建立：

- 内含子是独立主动信息宿主；
- 每一个基因或移动元件都是独立生命；
- 被动分子持久性已经满足生命定义；
- 内含子能够主动识别自身；
- 内含子把自身未来存在作为内在价值；
- 嵌套信息结构会自动构成嵌套生命。

### 当前生命边界判断

> 该移动内含子是一个嵌套、可迁移的生物信息结构。本研究显示了未来跨宿主连续性的一项前置条件，但没有证明该内含子已经独立跨越 IEH 中从“被承载的信息结构”到“主动信息宿主”的门槛。

## 10. Methodological Relevance for IEH Life-Boundary Research

This section records research directions only. It is not a complete biological theory.

### 10.1 Unit-of-analysis rule

IEH analysis should identify the proposed unit before asking whether it is alive:

- RNA molecule;
- intron sequence;
- all copies of an intron lineage;
- bacterial host;
- archaeal recipient;
- microbial community;
- host–element system.

Different units may have different continuity properties.

### 10.2 Carrier-versus-lineage rule

A single RNA molecule may be degraded while the sequence lineage persists through copies.

\[
\text{molecular carrier continuity}
\neq
\text{sequence-lineage continuity}
\]

Evidence notes should state which continuity is being measured.

### 10.3 Passive-versus-active rule

Persistence produced by chemical stability should be distinguished from persistence produced by feedback, repair, replication, or resource allocation.

\[
\text{passive stability}
\neq
\text{active maintenance}
\]

### 10.4 Host-benefit-versus-element-benefit rule

A molecular mechanism may be selected because it benefits the mobile element, the host, both, neither, or a larger ecological system. IER-related interpretation requires identifying the level at which maintenance is organized.

### 10.5 Continuity-completion rule

Cross-host continuity should not be claimed until evidence traces source identity, carrier transition, integration or copying, continued function or replication, and historical linkage across the transition.

## 10. 对 IEH 生命边界研究的方法论意义

本节只记录研究方向，不构成完整生物理论。

### 10.1 分析单位规则

在判断某对象是否是生命之前，IEH 分析必须先确定讨论单位：

- 单个 RNA 分子；
- 内含子序列；
- 一个内含子谱系的全部副本；
- 细菌宿主；
- 古菌接受者；
- 微生物群落；
- 宿主—元件复合系统。

不同单位可能具有不同的连续性属性。

### 10.2 载体连续性与谱系连续性规则

单个 RNA 分子可能被降解，但其序列谱系可以通过副本继续存在。

\[
\text{分子载体连续性}
\neq
\text{序列谱系连续性}
\]

证据笔记必须说明实际测量的是哪一种连续性。

### 10.3 被动持久性与主动维护规则

由化学稳定性产生的持久性，应与通过反馈、修复、复制或资源分配产生的持久性区分。

\[
\text{被动稳定}
\neq
\text{主动维护}
\]

### 10.4 宿主利益与元件利益规则

一种分子机制可能因为有利于移动元件、宿主、两者、两者都不是，或更大的生态系统而受到选择。与 IER 有关的解释必须识别维护行为在哪一个层级得到组织。

### 10.5 连续性完成规则

只有当证据能够追踪来源身份、载体转换、整合或复制、持续功能或繁殖，以及转换前后的历史连接时，才能主张跨宿主信息连续性已经完成。
