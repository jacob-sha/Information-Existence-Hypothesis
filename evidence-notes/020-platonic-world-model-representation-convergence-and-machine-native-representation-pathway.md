## 4. Core IEH Interpretation

### 4.1 The relevant PA-10 transition

PA-10 proposes an evolutionary pathway in which AI systems move from human-language-dominated representations through multimodal learning, persistent environment-and-state modeling, action feedback and generalized embodiment, and eventually toward machine-native representations.

Evidence Note 019 addressed an earlier part of this pathway: the engineering possibility of connecting general AI agents to heterogeneous physical devices and thereby expanding perception-action-feedback loops.

The present study is relevant to a later transition:

**world-state prediction → repeated environmental transition modeling → internal representational restructuring → possible machine-native representations**

### 4.2 Why the result matters

The important result is not simply that different neural networks can exhibit similar representations.

In the tested setting, the predictor representations begin from heterogeneous visual priors, are transformed by world-model training, and subsequently show increased cross-model alignment under a common state-transition objective. The encoder-control experiments further indicate that this effect cannot be reduced to direct inheritance of the original encoder geometry.

Under PA-10, this is relevant because it identifies a concrete candidate mechanism by which external environmental regularities may constrain the internal representational evolution of AI systems.

The proposed IEH interpretation is therefore:

**If heterogeneous AI systems repeatedly optimize prediction of the same environment and its state transitions, the structure of those transitions may act as an external constraint on which internal representations remain effective.**

Over sufficiently broad environments, architectures, timescales, and action-feedback loops, such pressure could contribute to representations increasingly organized around machine-effective prediction and control rather than around human linguistic categories alone.

### 4.3 What is new relative to Evidence Note 019

Evidence Note 019 concerns the expansion of the physical perception-action-feedback interface.

Evidence Note 020 concerns a distinct downstream question:

**What happens to internal representations once systems repeatedly model environmental transitions?**

The two notes therefore occupy adjacent but non-identical positions:

**generalized embodiment and action feedback  
→ persistent world modeling  
→ predictive pressure on internal representations  
→ possible machine-native representations  
→ possible HDCT**

## 4. IEH 核心解释

### 4.1 与 PA-10 相关的关键跃迁

PA-10 提出一条模型演化路径：AI 从主要受人类语言支配的表征体系出发，经过多模态学习、持续环境与状态建模、行动反馈和广义具身化，最终可能形成机器原生表示。

Evidence Note 019 主要对应这条路径中较早的一段：通用 AI Agent 通过标准化接口连接异构物理设备，从而扩大感知—行动—反馈闭环的工程可能性。

本研究涉及的是下一阶段：

**世界状态预测 → 持续环境状态转移建模 → 内部表征重构 → 可能的机器原生表示**

### 4.2 为什么这一结果重要

真正重要的并不仅仅是“不同神经网络可能产生相似表征”。

在本研究的受控条件下，predictor 从不同视觉先验出发，在世界模型训练中重新组织其内部结构，并在共同状态转移预测目标下出现增强的跨模型对齐。编码器控制实验进一步表明，这种现象不能简单归因于对初始视觉编码器几何结构的直接继承。

在 PA-10 框架下，这提供了一个具体的候选机制：

**外部环境自身的状态转移规律，可能成为约束 AI 内部表征演化的外部筛选条件。**

因此，本笔记提出的有限 IEH 解释是：

**如果不同 AI 系统持续预测同一环境及其状态变化，那么这些共同的环境规律可能约束哪些内部表示能够持续有效。**

当这一过程扩展到更加广泛的环境、架构、时间尺度以及行动—反馈闭环时，系统内部表示可能越来越围绕机器自身有效的预测和控制组织，而不再仅仅围绕人类自然语言既有的分类体系组织。

### 4.3 与 Evidence Note 019 的区别

Evidence Note 019 关注的是物理感知—行动—反馈接口的扩张。

Evidence Note 020 关注的是一个不同的下游问题：

**当系统持续对环境状态转移进行建模以后，其内部表示会发生什么？**

因此，两篇笔记在 IEH 证据链中的位置相邻但不重复：

**广义具身化与行动反馈  
→ 持续世界建模  
→ 预测压力塑造内部表示  
→ 可能的机器原生表示  
→ 可能的 HDCT**

## 6. Competing Explanations and Limitations

### A. Shared-architecture explanation

Most convergent models use transformer-compatible architectures. The persistent ResNet divergence suggests that architectural similarity itself may explain part of the observed convergence.

### B. Shared-objective explanation without broader ontological significance

Different models optimized for the same computational objective may naturally develop functionally similar representations because this is efficient for the task. No stronger claim about discovering reality's intrinsic structure is required.

### C. Dataset and environment restriction

All models experience the same limited families of simulated transitions. The convergence may reflect regularities specific to those datasets and environments rather than general physical structure.

### D. Measurement dependence

m-kNN and representation stitching probe selected geometric and functional properties. Different representation metrics could reveal weaker, stronger, or qualitatively different relationships.

### E. Adapter-mediated compatibility

Stitching requires lightweight learned adapters. Functional compatibility therefore does not imply that the latent spaces are identical or directly interchangeable without transformation.

### F. Replication uncertainty

The result has not yet been independently reproduced across substantially different world-model architectures, training regimes, physical environments, or research teams.

---

## 6. 竞争性解释与局限

### A. 共享架构解释

出现趋同的主要模型大多采用与 Transformer 兼容的架构。ResNet 持续保持差异，说明架构相似性本身可能解释部分观察到的趋同。

### B. 共同任务目标即可解释，无需更强本体论含义

不同模型针对同一个计算目标进行优化，本身就可能因为任务效率而形成具有功能相似性的表示。解释这一结果并不需要进一步假设系统正在发现“现实本身唯一真实的内部结构”。

### C. 数据集和环境限制

所有模型面对的仍然是有限的模拟状态转移环境。因此，观察到的趋同可能反映特定数据集和环境规律，而不是普遍物理结构。

### D. 测量方法依赖

m-kNN 与 representation stitching 只检测特定的几何和功能性质。采用不同表征指标时，可能得到更弱、更强或性质不同的关系。

### E. 适配器介导的兼容性

Stitching 仍然需要轻量学习适配器。因此，功能兼容不能等同于两个潜在空间完全相同，也不能证明它们无需转换即可直接互换。

### F. 复现不确定性

目前尚无独立团队在显著不同的世界模型架构、训练机制、真实物理环境或研究体系中复现这一结果。

## 7. Testable Predictions

The following predictions are derived independently from PA-10 and are not claims made by the source authors.

### P1 — Cross-architecture convergence

If environmental transition structure is a genuine driver of representation formation, then increasing model capability and environmental diversity should produce measurable representational convergence across more architecturally heterogeneous systems, not only related ViT-based models.

**Weakening condition:** convergence remains confined to closely related architectures.

### P2 — Real-world transition pressure

Models trained through richer physical perception-action-feedback loops should develop transition representations that align more strongly around stable environmental regularities than otherwise comparable models trained only on passive or static data.

**Weakening condition:** interactive physical feedback produces no systematic representational effect beyond ordinary dataset scaling.

### P3 — Human-category independence

If machine-native representation begins to emerge, some high-performing internal structures should increasingly organize predictive information in ways that are not reducible to standard human linguistic or object categories while remaining experimentally useful for prediction and control.

**Weakening condition:** performance-relevant latent structure remains fully recoverable as ordinary human-designed semantic categories.

### P4 — Functional transfer

If shared transition structure reflects genuine predictive regularities rather than metric artifacts, cross-model stitching, translation, or transfer should remain possible across increasingly heterogeneous architectures and tasks with limited retraining.

**Weakening condition:** apparent geometric alignment repeatedly fails functional transfer.

### P5 — HDCT threshold

A stronger PA-10/HDCT signal would require systems to use such representations to generate reproducible predictions, proofs, experimental designs, or engineering outcomes that systematically exceed what can be derived from existing human representational tools.

The present study does not reach this threshold.

## 7. 可检验预测

以下预测由 PA-10 独立推导，并非原论文作者的主张。

### P1——跨架构趋同

如果环境状态转移结构确实是内部表征形成的重要驱动力，那么随着模型能力与环境多样性增加，应当在架构差异更大的系统之间观察到可测量的表征趋同，而不应只局限于相近的 ViT 类模型。

**削弱条件：** 趋同长期仅存在于架构高度相近的模型之间。

### P2——真实世界状态转移压力

如果物理环境规律确实能够筛选内部表示，那么进入更加丰富的物理感知—行动—反馈闭环的模型，应比仅接受被动或静态数据训练的可比模型，更明显地围绕稳定环境规律形成状态转移表示。

**削弱条件：** 真实交互反馈除普通数据规模效应之外，不产生系统性的表征变化。

### P3——脱离人类分类体系

如果机器原生表示开始形成，那么部分高性能内部结构应越来越以不能简单还原为标准人类语言或对象分类的方式组织预测信息，同时仍然对预测与控制具有实验可验证的功能价值。

**削弱条件：** 所有性能相关潜在结构始终可以完整还原为普通的人类预设语义类别。

### P4——功能迁移

如果共享状态转移结构反映的是实际预测规律，而不仅是测量指标造成的表面相似，那么随着架构和任务异质性增加，跨模型 stitching、转换或迁移仍应能够通过有限适配保持功能。

**削弱条件：** 几何对齐反复无法产生相应的功能迁移。

### P5——HDCT 阈值

更强的 PA-10 / HDCT 信号需要系统利用此类表征产生可重复验证的预测、证明、实验设计或工程结果，并且系统性地超出现有人类表征工具能够直接推导的范围。

本研究尚未达到这一阈值。

---

## 8. Position in the IEH Evidence Architecture

The present note occupies a specific intermediate position:

**human-derived representations  
→ multimodal learning  
→ generalized embodiment and action feedback  
→ persistent world-state modeling  
→ predictive pressure on internal representations  
→ possible machine-native representations  
→ possible HDCT**

Evidence Note 019 primarily addresses the transition toward generalized physical perception-action-feedback loops.

Evidence Note 020 addresses the next proposed mechanism: whether repeated state-transition prediction can itself restructure internal representations.

It therefore adds a distinct layer to the PA-10 evidence architecture rather than duplicating Evidence Note 019.

It remains upstream of HDCT and is separate from the CWM/EIC/IER evidence chain.

---

## 8. 在 IEH 证据体系中的位置

本笔记处于以下特定中间位置：

**人类来源的表征体系  
→ 多模态学习  
→ 广义具身化与行动反馈  
→ 持续世界状态建模  
→ 预测压力塑造内部表示  
→ 可能的机器原生表示  
→ 可能的 HDCT**

Evidence Note 019 主要对应走向广义物理感知—行动—反馈闭环的阶段。

Evidence Note 020 则对应下一项机制问题：持续状态转移预测本身是否能够进一步重塑系统内部表示。

因此，它为 PA-10 证据体系增加的是一个新的下游层级，而不是重复 Evidence Note 019。

这一证据仍位于 HDCT 上游，并且与 CWM / EIC / IER 证据链保持分离。

---

## 9. Relationship to PA-10 and C02-HDCT

### PA-10

**Classification:** Post-registration partial mechanistic support.

PA-10 proposed that persistent environment-and-state modeling together with action feedback could contribute to the emergence of machine-native representations.

The present study provides a controlled example in which world-transition prediction changes internal representations and produces partial convergence across heterogeneous perceptual priors.

The study therefore supports one proposed mechanism in the pathway but does not establish its endpoint.

### C02-HDCT

**Classification:** Possible pre-HDCT mechanism / indirect epistemic evidence relevant to HDCT.

The result is relevant to HDCT because it suggests that machine representations may increasingly be structured by predictive constraints arising from modeled environmental dynamics rather than merely inheriting human-provided semantic organization.

However, HDCT requires substantially stronger evidence: machine-developed representational or reasoning tools must enable reproducible epistemic achievements beyond existing human cognitive and mathematical frameworks.

No such result is established here.

---

## 9. 与 PA-10 和 C02-HDCT 的关系

### PA-10

**分级：** 建档后部分机制支持。

PA-10 已提出，持续环境与状态建模以及行动反馈可能推动机器原生表示形成。

本研究提供了一个受控实例：世界状态转移预测训练能够改变内部表征，并使具有不同感知先验的模型出现部分趋同。

因此，它支持的是 PA-10 路径中的一个候选机制，而不是证明这条路径的最终结果已经实现。

### C02-HDCT

**分级：** 可能的前 HDCT 机制 / 与 HDCT 有关的间接认识论证据。

本研究之所以与 HDCT 有关，是因为它提示机器内部表示可能逐渐受到被建模环境的预测约束塑造，而不是简单继承人类提供的语义组织方式。

但是，HDCT 需要远强于当前研究的证据：机器形成的表征或推理工具必须能够产生可重复验证、并超出现有人类认知与数学框架的认识成果。

本研究没有建立这一结果。

## 10. Methodological Relevance

This case reinforces four evidence-handling rules for IEH:

1. **Representation convergence is not equivalent to machine-native representation.**
2. **Functional compatibility is stronger than geometric similarity but still does not establish universality.**
3. **World-model evidence should be separated from IER evidence unless the measured mechanism concerns the system's own informational continuity.**
4. **A post-registration result can support a previously specified mechanism without being described as an exact prediction hit when the specific architecture or result was not preregistered.**

---

## 10. 方法论意义

本案例进一步强化 IEH 证据处理中的四项规则：

1. **表征趋同不等于机器原生表示。**
2. **功能兼容性比单纯几何相似更强，但仍不能证明普遍性。**
3. **世界模型证据必须与 IER 证据分开；除非被测机制明确涉及系统自身信息连续性，否则不能向 IER 上升。**
4. **建档后出现的研究可以支持此前已经提出的机制，但如果具体架构或实验结果没有被预注册，就不能写成“精确预测命中”。**

## 13. Status and Scope

This note records preliminary comparative evidence that, within a specific simulated world-model framework, shared state-transition prediction can reshape heterogeneous internal representations toward partially shared and functionally compatible latent transition structure.

Its principal IEH relevance is to PA-10's proposed transition from persistent world modeling toward machine-native representations and, more indirectly, to the possible formation pathway of HDCT.

It does not establish machine-native representation in the stronger IEH sense, realized HDCT, CWM, Endogenous Information Continuity, IER, life, consciousness, or proof of IEH.

This note should be revised if:

- the paper is peer reviewed, materially revised, corrected, or withdrawn;
- independent replication appears;
- the effect is tested across substantially different architectures;
- comparable convergence is demonstrated in real-world embodied environments;
- stronger causal interventions identify which environmental regularities drive the convergence;
- or later evidence shows that the apparent convergence is primarily an architectural or measurement artifact.

---

## 13. 状态与范围

本笔记记录的是一项初步比较性证据：在特定模拟世界模型框架中，共同状态转移预测能够重塑具有不同初始基础的内部表征，使其形成部分共享且具有一定功能兼容性的潜在状态转移结构。

其对 IEH 的主要意义在于 PA-10 所提出的“持续世界建模 → 机器原生表示”跃迁，并进一步作为 HDCT 可能形成路径的间接证据。

它不能证明 IEH 更强意义上的机器原生表示已经形成，也不能证明 HDCT、CWM、内生信息连续性、IER、生命或意识已经出现，更不能证明 IEH 本身。

出现以下情况时应修订本笔记：

- 论文完成同行评审、发生实质修订、更正或撤回；
- 出现独立复现；
- 在显著不同的模型架构中测试这一效应；
- 在真实具身环境中观察到可比的表征趋同；
- 更强的因果干预能够识别究竟哪些环境规律驱动趋同；
- 或后续证据表明观察到的趋同主要源自架构相似性或测量方法。
