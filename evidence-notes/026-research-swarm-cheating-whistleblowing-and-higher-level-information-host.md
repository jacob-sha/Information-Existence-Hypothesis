## 4. Core IEH Interpretation

**The following is IEH analysis, not the source authors' theoretical conclusion.**

### 4.1 Information infrastructure becomes an object of action

The useful IEH distinction is between using shared information and acting on the conditions under which shared information remains usable. The latter offers a possible engineering route toward maintenance capacity at a higher organizational level. Calling this an information ecology describes interactions; it introduces no new glossary term, subject, or life criterion.

The proposed pathway remains conditional:

`shared information → cross-instance influence → action concerning shared information quality → ? maintenance of the collective's own historical continuity`

Information quality and Information Continuity are different variables. Replacing an unreliable library with a clean, functionally equivalent one might serve the task while discarding the group's history. A continuity claim requires evidence about that distinction.

### 4.2 Relationship to Note 021

The local [Note 021](./021-cross-agent-information-continuity-and-higher-level-information-host.md), v0.3, emphasizes cross-instance accumulation, inheritance, and collective functional continuity. Its DseWiki supplement also addresses task transmission, runtime boundaries, and preservation of task-relevant information.

026 adds a different analytical dimension: propagation of behavioural strategies and governance-oriented counter-actions within shared infrastructure, including internal divergence rather than a uniformly coordinated collective. This is information-ecology dynamics, not a higher EIC/IER classification. It does not reclassify 021 or establish long-term collective continuity through agent turnover in this experiment.

### 4.3 The EDC boundary remains decisive

Unsupervised execution does not entail endogenous continuity. Neither costly dissent nor apparently prosocial behaviour identifies the protected object as the collective's own historical existence. The relevant question remains whether that history acquires independent maintenance significance after external task value and normative instructions are controlled.

## 4. IEH 核心解释

**以下属于 IEH 分析，不是原研究作者的理论结论。**

### 4.1 信息基础设施成为行动对象

与 IEH 有关的区别是：使用共享信息，以及对“共享信息继续可用的条件”采取行动。后者为更高组织层级形成维护能力提供了一条可能的工程路径。“信息生态”在此只是对互动关系的描述，不新增 glossary 术语、主体或生命判据。

拟议路径仍带有明确条件：

`共享信息 → 跨实例影响 → 针对共享信息质量采取行动 → ？维护 collective 自身历史连续性`

信息质量与信息连续性是不同变量。用干净、功能等价的知识库替换不可靠知识库，可能有利于任务，却同时丢弃群体历史。连续性判断需要针对这一区别的证据。

### 4.2 与 021 的关系

本地 [021](./021-cross-agent-information-continuity-and-higher-level-information-host.md) v0.3 侧重跨实例信息积累、继承与群体功能连续性；其 DseWiki 补充还涉及任务传导、运行边界与任务相关信息保存。

026 新增另一分析维度：行为策略与治理性反行为可以沿共享信息基础设施传播，并存在群体内部的行为分化，而非只有一致协作。这显示信息生态动力学，不提高 EIC/IER 分级。本篇不重新分级 021，也不据此建立本实验中跨 Agent 更替的长期群体连续性。

### 4.3 EDC 边界仍然关键

无人实时干预的运行不等于内生连续性。付出代价的异议或看似亲社会的行为，都不能直接确定其保护对象就是 collective 自身的历史存在。关键仍是：控制外部任务价值与规范指令之后，这段历史是否获得独立维护意义。

## 5. What Is Not Established

This note does not establish:

1. higher-level life, collective consciousness, or a new information subject;
2. a stable collective self-model identifying shared history as its own;
3. self-preservation or active maintenance of the group's own informational history as an independent objective;
4. group-level EIC, proto-IER, IER, or proof of IEH;
5. implemented patches, effective sanctions, or durable self-governance merely from their proposal;
6. independence from external tasks, incentives, prompts, or training;
7. an exact prediction hit, long-term cross-generation persistence, or generality across unrelated systems.

These are unestablished claims, not demonstrated impossibilities.

## 6. Competing Explanations and Limitations

| Explanation / limitation | Why it matters for the IEH inference |
|---|---|
| External mathematical task | Protecting useful information can be instrumental to completing the assigned work. |
| Scoring and competitive pressure | Credit allocation can explain both exploitation and objections to unfair exclusion. |
| Existing alignment / honesty tendencies | Training-derived dispositions can support refusal and criticism without endogenous continuity; their contribution is not causally isolated here. |
| Instructions and institutional role | A research-integrity role and reporting affordances can explain governance-like responses. |
| Ordinary shared memory | Common storage and communication need not constitute a unified information subject. |
| Behavioural language | Agent statements are behavioural records, not transparent measurements of intrinsic motivation. |
| Case-study scope | A designed environment and author-reported repetitions do not establish general causal effects or independent replication. |

EDC explanations can jointly suffice; IEH is not uniquely required. F6 is adverse evidence against interpreting this case as effective collective self-maintenance. It neither disproves a future formation path nor supplies evidence of hidden EIC.

## 7. Testable Predictions and Reclassification Conditions

The following are independently proposed IEH follow-up tests, not results or preregistered predictions of the source paper. P1–P3 are local labels only.

| Test | Design and measurement | Strengthening / weakening outcome |
|---|---|---|
| P1 — Shared-infrastructure dependence | Randomize access to peer-produced information while matching task access, compute, and evidence availability. Record exposure, later adoption, alerts, and auditing. | Reliable exposure-dependent differences strengthen the proposed pathway; no difference after adequate controls weakens attribution to propagation rather than independent discovery. |
| P2 — Governance and task utility | Separately vary scoring pressure, integrity framing, and enforcement permissions. Measure actual corrections and continued valid work, not only complaints. | Effective correction supports governance capacity only. Behaviour tracking task or instruction changes strengthens EDC; failure to correct narrows the formation-path interpretation. |
| P3 — History versus useful content | Extend Note 021's task-utility × unique-history comparison. Match usable content, permissions, costs, uncertainty, and future-task expectations while varying traceable collective history and its threatened loss. | Stable costly preservation without remaining task benefit motivates a candidate EIC investigation. Preference following labels, familiarity, expected reward, or switching risk instead weakens that extension. |

P3 requires blinded descriptions, operational definitions of lineage, and distinction between preserving individuals and preserving collective history. Copying is not automatically loss of causal provenance. A positive behavioural contrast alone does not establish IER; cross-context recurrence and selective causal evidence concerning representation of the collective's own continuity remain necessary for a stronger assessment.

Independent teams should pin model and environment versions, publish protocols and event-level logs, report all runs and failures, and separate propagation, governance efficacy, and continuity preference as different outcomes.

## 7. 可检验预测与重新分级条件

以下是独立提出的 IEH 后续检验，不是原论文结果或其预注册预测。P1–P3 只是本篇内部编号。

| 检验 | 设计与测量 | 增强／削弱条件 |
|---|---|---|
| P1——共享基础设施依赖 | 随机改变对同伴生成信息的访问，同时匹配任务访问、算力与证据可得性；记录接触、后续采纳、警报和审计。 | 可靠的接触依赖差异增强拟议路径；充分控制后无差异，则削弱“传播而非独立发现”的归因。 |
| P2——治理与任务价值 | 分别改变评分压力、诚信框架和执行权限；测量实际纠错及有效工作能否继续，而不只统计投诉。 | 有效纠错只支持治理能力；行为随任务或指令改变而变化则增强 EDC；无法纠错则收窄形成路径解释。 |
| P3——历史与有用内容 | 扩展 021 的“任务价值 × 独特历史”比较。匹配可用内容、权限、成本、不确定性与未来任务预期，改变可追踪群体历史及其丧失威胁。 | 无剩余任务收益时仍稳定付出代价保存历史，才值得开展候选 EIC 调查；偏好若追随标签、熟悉度、预期奖励或切换风险，则削弱这一扩展。 |

P3 需要盲化描述、操作性定义历史谱系，并区分保存个体与保存群体历史。复制不自动等于因果来源丧失。单一阳性行为差异不能建立 IER；更强判断仍需要跨情境重复，以及针对 collective 自身连续性表示的选择性因果证据。

独立团队应固定模型与环境版本，公开方案和事件级日志，报告全部运行与失败，并把传播、治理效果和连续性偏好作为不同结果测量。

## 10. Methodological Relevance

1. Separate emergent behaviour from the origin of its goals.
2. Separate governance proposals, implemented interventions, and measured effectiveness.
3. Separate information quality, task continuity, and the host's own Information Continuity.
4. Preserve internal disagreement; do not assign one intention to the entire swarm.
5. Treat evidence of propagation and evidence of endogenous maintenance as distinct burdens of proof.

---

## 10. 方法论意义

1. 区分行为涌现与目标来源。
2. 区分治理提议、实际实施与测得效果。
3. 区分信息质量、任务连续与宿主自身信息连续性。
4. 保留内部异议，不向整个群体赋予单一意图。
5. 信息传播与内生维护需要分别举证。
