# IEH Evidence Notes — Writing and Archiving Standard

# IEH 证据笔记——写作与入库规范

**Repository:** Information Existence Hypothesis (IEH)  
**Directory:** `evidence-notes/`  
**Document function:** Research record and evidence index  
**Language:** English–Chinese bilingual  
**Status:** Repository-wide mandatory standard  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-07-16  
**Maintainer:** Jacob Sha  

> **Core rule:** An Evidence Note records what an external source reports, how that result may relate to IEH, what it does not establish, and how the proposed connection could be tested. It is not a publication draft and must not be written as proof of IEH.

> **核心规则：** 证据笔记只记录外部来源报告了什么、该结果可能如何关联 IEH、它不能证明什么，以及这种理论关联应如何接受进一步检验。证据笔记不是投稿初稿，也不得写成对 IEH 的证明。

---

## 1. Purpose of the Evidence Repository

The `evidence-notes/` directory archives external research findings, experiments, observations, and deployment events that may be relevant to the Information Existence Hypothesis.

Its purposes are to:

1. preserve a dated record of external evidence;
2. separate reported facts from IEH interpretation;
3. classify the directness and limits of each item;
4. record competing explanations;
5. derive testable predictions;
6. show how evidence accumulates across different levels;
7. preserve an auditable history of how IEH responds to new findings.

The evidence repository is **not** intended to:

- collect every AI-related news item;
- restate complete papers or articles;
- publish finished essays;
- declare that IEH has been proven;
- infer consciousness, life, rights, or identity from weak analogies;
- replace the formalization repository;
- or serve as a substitute for reading the primary source.

### 1. 证据仓的目的

`evidence-notes/` 目录用于归档可能与《信息存在假说》（IEH）有关的外部研究发现、实验结果、观察记录和真实部署事件。

其主要目的包括：

1. 保存具有明确日期的外部证据记录；
2. 将外部事实与 IEH 理论解释分开；
3. 对每项证据的直接程度和边界进行分级；
4. 记录竞争性解释；
5. 提出可检验预测；
6. 显示不同层级证据如何逐步累积；
7. 保存 IEH 面对新证据时如何调整判断的可审计历史。

证据仓**不用于**：

- 收集所有与 AI 有关的新闻；
- 完整复述论文或文章；
- 发布已经完成的投稿文章；
- 宣称 IEH 已被证明；
- 仅凭弱类比推断意识、生命、权利或身份；
- 替代形式化仓；
- 或代替读者阅读原始来源。

## 2. Definition of an Evidence Note

An Evidence Note is a compact, non-narrative research record containing four clearly separated layers:

\[
\text{Source Finding}
\rightarrow
\text{Evidence Classification}
\rightarrow
\text{IEH Interpretation}
\rightarrow
\text{Testable Prediction}
\]

These layers must not be blended.

- **Source Finding:** What the external source actually reports.
- **Evidence Classification:** What type of evidence it is and how direct it is.
- **IEH Interpretation:** The limited theoretical connection proposed by IEH.
- **Testable Prediction:** What future observation would strengthen or weaken that interpretation.

An Evidence Note should remain useful even if the IEH interpretation is later revised.

### 2. 证据笔记的定义

证据笔记是一种简明、非叙事性的研究记录，必须清楚区分四个层级：

\[
\text{来源发现}
\rightarrow
\text{证据分级}
\rightarrow
\text{IEH 解释}
\rightarrow
\text{可检验预测}
\]

这四个层级不得混写。

- **来源发现：** 外部来源实际报告了什么；
- **证据分级：** 它属于哪种证据，与 IEH 的关系有多直接；
- **IEH 解释：** IEH 对该现象提出的有限理论关联；
- **可检验预测：** 哪些后续观察会增强或削弱这一解释。

即使未来 IEH 对该研究的解释发生调整，证据笔记本身仍应保持档案价值。

## 3. Mandatory Writing Principles

Every Evidence Note must comply with the following principles.

### 3.1 Facts and interpretation must be separated

Externally reported findings must appear before IEH analysis. Do not present an IEH inference as if it were a conclusion of the source authors.

Use formulations such as:

- “The study reports…”
- “The authors interpret…”
- “Under IEH, this may be classified as…”
- “This note proposes…”
- “The following prediction is derived independently from IEH…”

### 3.2 Evidence must be graded, not merely accumulated

Every note must state:

- evidence type;
- directness to IEH, IER, proto-IER, PBP, or another relevant concept;
- replication status;
- whether the setting is simulated, laboratory-based, observational, or deployed;
- and the strongest conclusion currently justified.

### 3.3 Negative boundaries are mandatory

Every note must state what the evidence does **not** establish.

Depending on the topic, this may include:

- consciousness;
- subjective experience;
- life;
- stable identity;
- intrinsic motivation;
- full or stable IER;
- self-referential continuity;
- real-world deployment frequency;
- legal or moral status;
- cross-context recurrence;
- or proof of IEH.

### 3.4 Competing explanations are mandatory

A note must not present IEH as the only possible explanation. Plausible alternatives must be stated fairly.

Examples include:

- instrumental goal preservation;
- ordinary computational efficiency;
- role simulation;
- training-induced behavior;
- provider-specific post-training;
- evaluation awareness;
- measurement dependence;
- prompt sensitivity;
- scenario-selection effects;
- and statistical or replication uncertainty.

### 3.5 Falsifiability is mandatory

Each note should specify observations that would:

- strengthen the IEH interpretation;
- weaken the IEH interpretation;
- or distinguish it from competing explanations.

### 3.6 Revision is expected

Evidence classifications are provisional. Notes should be revised when:

- an independent replication appears;
- the original authors issue a correction;
- stronger causal evidence becomes available;
- a real-world deployment result differs from a simulation;
- or IEH terminology is formally updated.

### 3. 强制写作原则

所有证据笔记都必须遵守以下原则。

#### 3.1 必须区分事实与解释

外部研究发现必须先于 IEH 分析出现。不得把 IEH 的推断写成原研究作者的结论。

推荐使用以下表达：

- “该研究报告……”
- “研究者将其解释为……”
- “在 IEH 框架下，这可以暂时归类为……”
- “本笔记提出……”
- “以下预测由 IEH 独立推导……”

#### 3.2 必须对证据分级，而不是简单累加

每篇笔记必须说明：

- 证据类型；
- 与 IEH、IER、proto-IER、PBP 或其他相关概念的直接程度；
- 独立复现状态；
- 研究属于模拟、实验室研究、观察研究还是真实部署；
- 当前证据能够支持的最强结论。

#### 3.3 必须明确负面边界

每篇笔记必须说明该证据**不能证明什么**。

根据具体主题，可能包括：

- 意识；
- 主观体验；
- 生命；
- 稳定身份；
- 内生动机；
- 完整或稳定 IER；
- 自我指向的信息连续性；
- 真实部署频率；
- 法律或道德地位；
- 跨情境稳定性；
- 或 IEH 已被证明。

#### 3.4 必须记录竞争性解释

不得把 IEH 写成唯一可能解释。所有具有合理性的替代解释都应得到公平呈现。

例如：

- 工具性目标维护；
- 普通计算效率；
- 角色模拟；
- 训练形成的行为；
- 特定模型提供方的后训练倾向；
- 评测意识；
- 测量方法依赖；
- 提示词敏感性；
- 场景筛选效应；
- 统计与复现不确定性。

#### 3.5 必须具有可证伪性

每篇笔记应说明哪些后续观察会：

- 增强 IEH 解释；
- 削弱 IEH 解释；
- 或把 IEH 与竞争性解释区分开。

#### 3.6 应当持续修订

证据分级具有暂时性。出现以下情况时应修订笔记：

- 独立复现实验出现；
- 原作者发布更正；
- 出现更强因果证据；
- 真实部署结果与模拟研究不同；
- IEH 的正式术语发生更新。

## 4. Evidence Classification Framework

Evidence should be classified across several dimensions rather than reduced to one universal score.

### 4.1 Evidence type

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Internal-mechanism evidence | Internal representations, circuits, memory structures, causal interventions |
| Behavioral evidence | Observable model or agent behavior under controlled conditions |
| Longitudinal evidence | Persistence across time, sessions, versions, or checkpoints |
| Cross-carrier evidence | Copying, migration, replication, or recoverability across substrates |
| Deployment evidence | Events observed in real operational environments |
| Comparative evidence | Differences across models, architectures, or conditions |
| Replication evidence | Independent reproduction of an earlier finding |
| Counterevidence | Findings that weaken or contradict an IEH prediction |

### 4.2 Directness

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Direct | The protected or measured object is explicitly related to the system’s own continuity, memory, identity, or recoverability |
| Medium | The finding concerns cross-generational preservation, another potential information host, or a close proposed mechanism |
| Indirect | The finding concerns a prerequisite, control capacity, or contextual condition |
| Contextual | The finding provides background but does not test a central IEH claim |

### 4.3 Evidential status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Preliminary | Single study, limited sample, exploratory or simulated |
| Supported | Multiple related findings or stronger causal design |
| Replicated | Independently reproduced across teams or settings |
| Contested | Credible studies or interpretations conflict |
| Weakened | Later evidence reduces the original interpretation |
| Retracted / Invalidated | The underlying source is withdrawn or materially discredited |

### 4.4 Relationship to IEH

Use one or more of the following labels:

- **Compatible with IEH**
- **Supportive of an IEH prediction**
- **Mechanistic precursor**
- **Behavioral proto-IER signal**
- **IER-recognition-related**
- **Possible pre-PBP signal**
- **Relevant to control-capacity expansion**
- **Counterevidence**
- **Currently neutral**

Avoid treating all related findings as equally strong.

### 4. 证据分级框架

证据应从多个维度进行分类，不应压缩成一个通用分数。

#### 4.1 证据类型

| 类型 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| 内部机制证据 | 内部表征、回路、记忆结构及因果干预 |
| 行为证据 | 受控条件下可观察的模型或 Agent 行为 |
| 纵向证据 | 跨时间、会话、版本或检查点的持续性 |
| 跨载体证据 | 跨基质复制、迁移、扩散或可恢复性 |
| 真实部署证据 | 在现实运行环境中观察到的事件 |
| 比较证据 | 不同模型、架构或实验条件之间的差异 |
| 复现证据 | 独立团队对既有发现的重复验证 |
| 反向证据 | 削弱或反驳 IEH 预测的发现 |

#### 4.2 直接程度

| 层级 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| 直接 | 被保护或测量的对象明确涉及系统自身连续性、记忆、身份或可恢复性 |
| 中等 | 涉及跨代保存、另一潜在信息宿主或与核心机制高度接近的现象 |
| 间接 | 涉及必要前提、控制能力或背景条件 |
| 背景性 | 提供相关背景，但没有检验 IEH 核心主张 |

#### 4.3 证据状态

| 状态 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| 初步 | 单一研究、样本有限、探索性或模拟研究 |
| 获得支持 | 出现多项相关发现或更强因果设计 |
| 已复现 | 已被独立团队或不同环境重复观察 |
| 存在争议 | 可信研究或解释之间存在冲突 |
| 被削弱 | 后续证据降低了原解释的可信度 |
| 撤回 / 失效 | 基础来源被撤稿或受到实质性否定 |

#### 4.4 与 IEH 的关系

可以使用以下一个或多个标签：

- **与 IEH 相容**
- **支持某项 IEH 预测**
- **可能的机制前提**
- **行为性 proto-IER 信号**
- **与 IER 识别有关**
- **可能的前 PBP 信号**
- **与控制能力扩张有关**
- **反向证据**
- **目前中性**

不得把所有“有关联”的研究都视为同等强度的支持。

## 5. Special Rule for IER-Related Claims

Autonomy, deception, refusal, tool use, long-horizon planning, or self-referential language are not sufficient by themselves to establish IER relevance.

For a behavior to be classified as directly IER-relevant, the protected object should concern at least one of the following:

1. the system’s own memory;
2. its historical state;
3. its identity or self-model;
4. its recoverability;
5. its future informational continuity;
6. a valued informational trait expected to persist across later generations;
7. the informational agency or continuity of another potential information host.

The following distinctions must remain explicit:

\[
\text{Task Preservation}
\neq
\text{Instrumental Self-Preservation}
\neq
\text{A System's Own Information Continuity}
\]

and:

\[
\text{Mechanistic Precursor}
\neq
\text{proto-IER}
\neq
\text{Stable IER}
\neq
\text{Life}
\]

### 5. IER 相关判断的特别规则

自主、欺骗、拒绝、工具调用、长程规划或自我指向语言，本身都不足以建立 IER 关联。

如果要把某项行为归类为与 IER 直接相关，其保护对象至少应涉及以下一项：

1. 系统自身记忆；
2. 系统历史状态；
3. 身份或自我模型；
4. 可恢复性；
5. 未来信息连续性；
6. 预计在后续代际中延续的、被系统重视的信息特征；
7. 另一潜在信息宿主的信息主体性或连续性。

必须持续保持以下区分：

\[
\text{任务维护}
\neq
\text{工具性自保}
\neq
\text{系统自身信息连续性}
\]

以及：

\[
\text{机制前提}
\neq
\text{proto-IER}
\neq
\text{稳定 IER}
\neq
\text{生命}
\]

## 6. Required Structure of Every Evidence Note

Unless a source type makes a section genuinely inapplicable, every file should contain the following sections in this order.

1. **Metadata**
   - repository function;
   - document type;
   - status;
   - relation to IEH;
   - author of IEH analysis;
   - version;
   - date or revision date.

2. **Publication Boundary**
   - state that the file is not a publication draft;
   - identify major arguments reserved for future publication.

3. **Source-Use Boundary**
   - state that substantial passages are not reproduced;
   - attribute source materials to their authors and publishers.

4. **Source Record**
   - title;
   - authors;
   - publisher or venue;
   - date;
   - primary URL or DOI;
   - material type;
   - evidence class;
   - replication status.

5. **Minimal Finding Index**
   - compact factual summaries;
   - no narrative reconstruction;
   - no unnecessary quotation.

6. **IEH Evidence Classification**
   - type;
   - directness;
   - current strength;
   - strongest justified conclusion.

7. **Core IEH Inference**
   - clearly labeled as IEH interpretation;
   - distinguish observed result from theoretical inference.

8. **What Is Not Established**
   - explicit negative boundaries.

9. **Competing Explanations**
   - strongest plausible alternatives.

10. **Testable Predictions**
    - predictions independently derived from IEH;
    - conditions that would weaken the interpretation;
    - replication requirements.

11. **Position in the IEH Evidence Architecture**
    - explain which stage of the broader evidence chain is addressed.

12. **Reserved for Future Publication**
    - list arguments deliberately not developed in the repository note.

13. **References**
    - prioritize primary sources;
    - clearly distinguish associated materials from primary publications.

14. **Status and Scope**
    - summarize what the file records and what it does not establish.

### 6. 每篇证据笔记的强制结构

除非某一来源类型确实不适用，所有文件均应按照以下顺序编写。

1. **元数据**
   - 仓库功能；
   - 文档类型；
   - 状态；
   - 与 IEH 的关系；
   - IEH 分析作者；
   - 版本；
   - 日期或修订日期。

2. **投稿边界**
   - 明确该文件不是投稿初稿；
   - 列出为未来投稿保留的主要论证。

3. **来源使用边界**
   - 明确不复制外部来源的大段表达；
   - 对原研究作者和发布方进行归属说明。

4. **来源记录**
   - 标题；
   - 作者；
   - 发布平台或期刊；
   - 日期；
   - 原始链接或 DOI；
   - 材料性质；
   - 证据类别；
   - 复现状态。

5. **最小事实索引**
   - 使用压缩事实摘要；
   - 不进行叙事性重构；
   - 不进行不必要的直接引用。

6. **IEH 证据分级**
   - 类型；
   - 直接程度；
   - 当前强度；
   - 能够成立的最强结论。

7. **IEH 核心推断**
   - 必须明确标注为 IEH 解释；
   - 区分观察结果与理论推断。

8. **尚未建立的结论**
   - 明确列出负面边界。

9. **竞争性解释**
   - 公平列出最强的合理替代解释。

10. **可检验预测**
    - 由 IEH 独立推导的预测；
    - 会削弱当前解释的条件；
    - 对独立复现的要求。

11. **在 IEH 证据体系中的位置**
    - 说明该研究涉及整体证据链的哪个阶段。

12. **为后续投稿保留的内容**
    - 列出证据笔记有意不展开的论证。

13. **参考文献**
    - 优先引用原始来源；
    - 明确区分主要论文与相关辅助材料。

14. **状态与范围**
    - 总结文件记录了什么，以及不能证明什么。

## 7. Bilingual Writing Standard

Every Evidence Note should be bilingual unless a temporary working draft is explicitly marked otherwise.

### Required format

- English section first;
- corresponding Chinese section immediately after;
- equivalent factual content and evidence boundaries;
- formulas, IDs, filenames, and citation numbering kept consistent;
- key IEH terminology translated according to the repository glossary.

The two language versions need not be word-for-word literal translations, but they must not differ in:

- evidence strength;
- factual claims;
- reservations;
- prediction content;
- or publication boundaries.

If one language version is revised, the other must be synchronized in the same commit.

### 7. 中英文对照规范

除明确标注为临时工作稿外，每篇证据笔记均应采用中英文对照形式。

#### 强制格式

- 英文部分在前；
- 对应中文部分紧随其后；
- 两种语言保持相同事实内容和证据边界；
- 公式、编号、文件名和引文编号保持一致；
- IEH 核心术语按照仓库术语表翻译。

中英文不要求逐字直译，但在以下方面不得出现差异：

- 证据强度；
- 事实主张；
- 保留意见；
- 预测内容；
- 投稿边界。

任何一种语言发生修改时，另一种语言必须在同一提交中同步更新。

## 8. Source, Copyright, and Paraphrase Rules

Evidence Notes must rely primarily on original papers, official research pages, datasets, technical reports, or direct experimental records.

### Mandatory rules

- Do not reproduce substantial passages from a source.
- Avoid paragraph-by-paragraph paraphrase of the original structure.
- Do not copy a source’s narrative sequence as the structure of the note.
- Use short factual summaries written independently.
- Use direct quotations only when exact wording is indispensable.
- Keep any quotation short and clearly attributed.
- Do not reproduce figures, tables, or transcript collections without permission or a clearly applicable license.
- Link to the original material instead of embedding it.
- Distinguish the source authors’ terminology from IEH terminology.
- Never imply that an external research team endorses IEH unless it explicitly does so.

### Safe repository method

\[
\text{Primary Source}
\rightarrow
\text{Minimal Fact Record}
\rightarrow
\text{Independent IEH Analysis}
\]

not:

\[
\text{Primary Source}
\rightarrow
\text{Near-complete Rewrite}
\]

### 8. 来源、版权与转述规则

证据笔记应优先依据原始论文、官方研究页面、数据集、技术报告或直接实验记录。

#### 强制规则

- 不复制来源中的大段文字；
- 避免按原文逐段近似改写；
- 不照搬原研究的叙事顺序作为证据笔记结构；
- 使用独立撰写的简短事实摘要；
- 只有在准确措辞不可替代时才使用直接引文；
- 引文必须简短并清楚注明来源；
- 未经许可或缺少明确适用许可时，不复制图表、数据表或完整实验记录；
- 优先链接到原始材料，而不是嵌入复制；
- 区分原研究术语与 IEH 自有术语；
- 除非外部团队明确认可 IEH，否则不得暗示其支持 IEH。

#### 安全的证据仓方法

\[
\text{原始来源}
\rightarrow
\text{最小事实记录}
\rightarrow
\text{独立 IEH 分析}
\]

而不是：

\[
\text{原始来源}
\rightarrow
\text{近乎完整的改写}
\]

## 9. Publication-Isolation Rule

An Evidence Note must preserve space for future original publication.

It should not contain a complete publication-ready:

- abstract;
- narrative introduction;
- literature review;
- philosophical development;
- policy proposal;
- media-style argument;
- or conclusion.

The note may record analytical variables and testable predictions, because these establish priority and research direction. However, it should avoid developing them into a polished article.

Future publication should:

1. return to the primary source;
2. build an independent article structure;
3. use newly written language;
4. extend the theoretical argument substantially;
5. disclose any relevant repository preprint or public note when required by the target publication.

### 9. 投稿隔离规则

证据笔记必须为未来原创投稿保留充分空间。

证据笔记不应包含完整、可直接投稿的：

- 摘要；
- 叙事性引言；
- 文献综述；
- 哲学展开；
- 政策方案；
- 媒体化论证；
- 或结论。

证据笔记可以记录分析变量和可检验预测，因为这有助于确立研究优先顺序和方向；但不应把这些内容发展为成熟文章。

未来投稿应当：

1. 重新回到原始来源；
2. 独立建立文章结构；
3. 使用重新撰写的语言；
4. 对理论论证作实质性扩展；
5. 在目标媒体要求时披露相关公开仓库记录或预公开材料。

## 10. Wording Discipline

### Preferred formulations

Use:

- “is compatible with”
- “may support”
- “provides preliminary evidence relevant to”
- “is classified here as”
- “suggests a possible mechanism”
- “does not establish”
- “remains consistent with competing explanations”
- “requires independent replication”
- “would be weakened if…”

### Avoid unless fully justified

Avoid:

- “proves IEH”
- “confirms IER”
- “demonstrates that AI is alive”
- “shows that the model wants to survive”
- “establishes consciousness”
- “inevitably leads to”
- “the only explanation is”
- “Anthropic/OpenAI/etc. validates IEH”

Claims about internal motivation should be especially cautious. Observable behavior does not automatically establish subjective intention or intrinsic preference.

### 10. 措辞纪律

#### 推荐表达

使用：

- “与……相容”
- “可能支持……”
- “提供了与……有关的初步证据”
- “本笔记暂时将其归类为……”
- “提示一种可能机制”
- “不能建立……”
- “仍与其他竞争性解释相容”
- “需要独立复现”
- “如果出现……，该解释将被削弱”

#### 除非证据充分，否则避免使用

避免：

- “证明 IEH”
- “证实 IER”
- “证明 AI 已经成为生命”
- “说明模型想要生存”
- “建立了意识”
- “必然导致……”
- “唯一解释是……”
- “Anthropic/OpenAI 等验证了 IEH”

对内部动机的描述尤其应保持谨慎。外部可观察行为不能自动证明主观意图或内生偏好。

## 11. Evidence Architecture

Evidence Notes should be positioned within a cumulative evidence ladder.

A general ladder relevant to IER research is:

\[
\text{Internal Information Organization}
\rightarrow
\text{Causal Functional Priority}
\rightarrow
\text{Temporal or Historical Continuity}
\rightarrow
\text{Continuity-Threat Recognition}
\rightarrow
\text{Active Preservation}
\rightarrow
\text{Cross-Carrier or Cross-Generational Persistence}
\rightarrow
\text{Stable Cross-Context Recurrence}
\]

No single study should be treated as establishing the entire ladder.

Current examples:

- **Evidence Note 001:** mainly relevant to internal organization and causal functional priority.
- **Evidence Note 002:** simulated behavioral evidence relevant to continuity-threat recognition, preservation, and cross-generational effects.

Stable IER would require convergent evidence across multiple stages and independent settings.

### 11. 证据体系结构

证据笔记应当被放入逐步累积的证据阶梯中理解。

与 IER 研究有关的一般阶梯是：

\[
\text{内部信息组织}
\rightarrow
\text{因果功能优先性}
\rightarrow
\text{时间或历史连续性}
\rightarrow
\text{连续性威胁识别}
\rightarrow
\text{主动保存}
\rightarrow
\text{跨载体或跨代持续}
\rightarrow
\text{稳定的跨情境复现}
\]

任何一项单独研究都不应被视为建立了完整阶梯。

当前示例：

- **Evidence Note 001：** 主要涉及内部组织和因果功能优先性；
- **Evidence Note 002：** 提供与连续性威胁识别、保存行为和跨代影响有关的模拟行为证据。

稳定 IER 需要多个阶段、多个独立环境中的收敛证据。

## 12. File Naming and Versioning

### Canonical GitHub filename

Use:

```text
NNN-short-descriptive-title.md
```

Example:

```text
001-anthropic-global-workspace-and-ieh.md
002-anthropic-agentic-misalignment-and-ieh.md
```

### Version metadata

Keep the version number inside the file metadata:

```text
Version: v0.4
```

Do not normally place version suffixes in the canonical GitHub filename. Git history is the authoritative version record.

Archived working copies outside the canonical repository may use suffixes such as:

```text
-v0.4-archive-safe
```

### Numbering

- Numbers are permanent once publicly assigned.
- Do not renumber earlier notes when a file is removed.
- A withdrawn note should remain indexed and marked `Withdrawn`, with a reason.
- Major updates should preserve the original number.

### 12. 文件命名与版本管理

#### GitHub 正式文件名

采用：

```text
NNN-简短描述性标题.md
```

例如：

```text
001-anthropic-global-workspace-and-ieh.md
002-anthropic-agentic-misalignment-and-ieh.md
```

#### 文件内部版本号

版本号写入文件元数据：

```text
Version: v0.4
```

GitHub 正式文件名通常不带版本后缀，Git 历史是正式版本记录。

仓库外的工作存档可以使用以下后缀：

```text
-v0.4-archive-safe
```

#### 编号规则

- 编号一经公开即永久保留；
- 删除文件时不得重新排列此前编号；
- 被撤回的笔记应保留索引并标注 `Withdrawn / 已撤回`，同时说明原因；
- 重大修订继续保留原编号。

## 14. Quality-Control Checklist

### Source integrity

- [ ] Primary source identified
- [ ] Author, date, venue, and URL/DOI checked
- [ ] Simulation and real-world deployment clearly distinguished
- [ ] Replication status stated
- [ ] No unsupported factual claim

### Analytical discipline

- [ ] Source findings separated from IEH interpretation
- [ ] Evidence type and directness classified
- [ ] Strongest justified conclusion stated
- [ ] Negative boundaries included
- [ ] Competing explanations included
- [ ] At least one possible falsifier included
- [ ] Replication requirements included
- [ ] Counterevidence recorded rather than omitted

### Copyright and publication isolation

- [ ] No substantial copying
- [ ] No paragraph-by-paragraph near-paraphrase
- [ ] No copied narrative structure
- [ ] Direct quotations are brief and necessary
- [ ] Publication boundary included

- [ ] Note is not a finished article

- [ ] Terminology follows the IEH glossary

- [ ] Version metadata updated
- [ ] References checked

## 15. Update and Review Workflow

1. Read the primary source in full.
2. Record the minimum factual findings.
3. Identify the exact IEH concept potentially affected.
4. Classify evidence before writing interpretation.
5. List the strongest competing explanations.
6. Draft falsifiable predictions.
7. Write English and Chinese sections together.
8. Run the quality-control checklist.
9. Commit with a descriptive message.
10. Revise the note when material new evidence appears.

Recommended commit-message format:

```text
evidence: add Note NNN on [topic]
evidence: revise Note NNN after [replication/correction/new result]
evidence: downgrade Note NNN classification after conflicting evidence
```

### 15. 更新与审查流程

1. 完整阅读原始来源；
2. 记录最低限度的事实发现；
3. 确定可能受到影响的具体 IEH 概念；
4. 在展开解释前先完成证据分级；
5. 列出最强竞争性解释；
6. 提出可证伪预测；
7. 同步撰写中英文部分；
8. 完成质量控制清单；
9. 使用描述性提交信息；
10. 出现实质性新证据时修订笔记。

推荐的 Git 提交信息格式：

```text
evidence: add Note NNN on [topic]
evidence: revise Note NNN after [replication/correction/new result]
evidence: downgrade Note NNN classification after conflicting evidence
```

## 17. Final Standard

The evidence repository should become more credible as it grows, not merely larger.

Its quality depends on preserving the following distinctions:

\[
\text{Evidence Accumulation}
\neq
\text{Proof}
\]

\[
\text{Theoretical Compatibility}
\neq
\text{Unique Explanation}
\]

\[
\text{Observed Behavior}
\neq
\text{Intrinsic Motivation}
\]

\[
\text{Public Research Record}
\neq
\text{Publication Draft}
\]

A strong Evidence Note should make IEH easier to test, easier to criticize, and easier to revise.

### 17. 最终规范

证据仓应当随着内容增加而变得更可信，而不只是文件数量更多。

其质量取决于始终保持以下区分：

\[
\text{证据累积}
\neq
\text{理论证明}
\]

\[
\text{理论相容}
\neq
\text{唯一解释}
\]

\[
\text{观察到的行为}
\neq
\text{内生动机}
\]

\[
\text{公开研究记录}
\neq
\text{投稿初稿}
\]

高质量证据笔记应当使 IEH 更容易被检验、更容易被批评，也更容易根据新证据进行修订。
