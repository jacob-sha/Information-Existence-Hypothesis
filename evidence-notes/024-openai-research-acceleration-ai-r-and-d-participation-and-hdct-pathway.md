## 4. Core IEH Interpretation

### 4.1 AI becomes part of the process that develops AI

**The following is IEH interpretation, not the source's theoretical conclusion.** The relevant organizational change is that AI systems increasingly participate in producing the code, analyses, or other research work used within AI development. AI is therefore both a research object and a contributor to the surrounding research process.

The limited pathway is:

`human-defined research objectives → agent-assisted research work → human evaluation and integration → possible improvements to later systems`

The final arrow requires outcome-level evidence. The existence of the workflow does not measure the improvement it causes. “AI participating in its own technological evolution” refers here to the broader technology lineage, not proof that one model autonomously rewrites and improves itself.

### 4.2 Three thresholds must remain separate

1. **Activity threshold:** agent runtime becomes large relative to recorded human hours.
2. **Contribution threshold:** validated, quality-adjusted research contribution becomes large relative to human contribution.
3. **Autonomy threshold:** systems independently determine and sustain a research-improvement cycle.

Evidence for the first cannot substitute for the second or third. Human research judgment may remain a bottleneck even when machine activity dominates elapsed agent time.

### 4.3 Communication is a downstream hypothesis

A future hypothesis is that sufficiently extensive agent collaboration could favor more efficient machine-oriented exchange formats. The paired PA-13 record develops this prediction separately; it is not a finding in F1–F5.

The missing links are:

`more agent activity → ? more task-relevant agent-to-agent exchange → ? a measured natural-language bottleneck → ? advantage from an alternative representation → ? stable machine-native communication`

Concurrency alone does not establish communication among agents. Compressed text, JSON, code, or a human-designed protocol does not by itself establish a new machine-native language. Nor does an efficiency incentive make such a transition inevitable: interoperability, auditing, and human coordination may favor readable interfaces.

## 4. IEH 核心解释

### 4.1 AI 成为开发 AI 的过程的一部分

**以下属于 IEH 解释，不是来源的理论结论。** 相关组织变化在于：AI 系统越来越多地参与生成 AI 研发中使用的代码、分析或其他研究工作。因此，AI 同时成为研究对象与周边研究过程的参与者。

有限路径是：

`人类定义研发目标 → Agent 辅助研究工作 → 人类评估与整合 → 后续系统可能获得改进`

最后一步需要成果层证据。工作流存在，不等于已测量它导致的改进。“AI 参与自身技术演化”在这里指较广泛的技术谱系，不是证明单个模型能够自主重写并改进自己。

### 4.2 三种阈值必须分开

1. **活动量阈值：** Agent 运行时长相对于记录的人类工时变大。
2. **贡献阈值：** 经验证、质量调整后的研究贡献相对于人类贡献变大。
3. **自主性阈值：** 系统独立确定并维持研究改进循环。

第一项证据不能替代第二项或第三项。即使机器活动在累计运行时间上占主导，人类研究判断仍可能是瓶颈。

### 4.3 通信属于下游假说

一项未来假说是：当 Agent 协作充分扩大时，可能出现采用更高效机器导向交换形式的压力。配套 PA-13 独立记录这一预测；它不是 F1–F5 的研究发现。

仍缺少的环节是：

`Agent 活动增加 → ？更多任务相关的 Agent 间交换 → ？测得自然语言瓶颈 → ？替代表征具有优势 → ？稳定机器原生通信`

并发本身不能证明 Agent 相互通信。压缩文本、JSON、代码或人类设计协议，也不能单独证明新型机器原生语言。效率动机不使这种转变成为必然：互操作、审计与人类协作可能继续推动可读接口。

## 6. Competing Explanations and Limitations

| Explanation / limitation | Implication for interpretation |
|---|---|
| Compute and parallelism | More available machine time can explain more activity without improved efficiency per unit resource. |
| Tool adoption and workflow redesign | Better access, interfaces, and delegation practices may increase use independently of a new cognitive mechanism. |
| Task selection | Researchers may delegate tasks already suited to agents; observed outcomes need not generalize to the full research pipeline. |
| Human complementary labor | Planning, evaluation, correction, and integration can supply decisive contributions not captured by agent runtime. |
| Measurement composition | Definitions, coverage, concurrency accounting, and support-role inclusion can alter ratios without an equivalent change in effective contribution. |
| Outcome selection and classifier error | Excluding uncertain outcomes and estimating task properties with models can change apparent success; calibration and missingness need audit. |
| Institutional self-report | A single laboratory's observations lack independent replication and may reflect its incentives or unusual infrastructure. |
| Ordinary protocol optimization | Future communication gains could come from better human-designed interfaces rather than machine-native representation. |

These explanations may coexist with genuine research acceleration. Their role is to limit causal and theoretical attribution, not to dismiss the operational change.

## 7. Testable Predictions and Reclassification Conditions

**These are IEH-derived follow-up tests, not completed experiments.**

### P1 — From activity to validated contribution

If expanding agent participation improves research productivity, matched projects should show better independently assessed outcomes per total resource, after accounting for human review, failed experiments, and compute.

**Upgrade:** Preregistered or credible controlled comparisons, independently audited across teams.

**Weaken / downgrade:** Activity rises while validated output stagnates, or gains disappear after resource and selection controls. This weakens productivity claims without denying increased use.

### P2 — From participation to a causal improvement loop

Trace an agent-produced research contribution into a later model; evaluate the resulting model against a matched version without that contribution; then test whether the improvement increases subsequent research effectiveness.

**Upgrade:** Repeated, attributable gains across successive iterations with full resource accounting.

**Weaken / downgrade:** Contributions fail integration, improvements do not transfer to research, or human/compute changes explain the effect. Participation remains below autonomous recursive self-improvement even if a supervised feedback loop is verified.

### P3 — Communication structure before communication-language claims

Measure task-relevant traffic among agents separately from human prompts, tool calls, repeated logs, and shared-storage reads. Compare human-readable and alternative communication conditions under matched budgets, task content, and coordination requirements.

**Upgrade toward a communication hypothesis:** Repeated causal efficiency gains from a learned exchange representation, with transfer to unfamiliar partners or tasks and evidence that standard protocols do not explain the gain.

**Weaken / downgrade:** There is no language bottleneck, apparent gains are serialization or compression effects, or a human-designed protocol matches performance. More traffic alone does not satisfy the test.

### P4 — HDCT-specific threshold

A stronger HDCT claim requires an identified cognitive tool with stable advantage, transfer, repeated external verification, and evidence of exceeding existing unaided human cognitive operation or reconstruction. Internal-representation interventions should distinguish mechanism from general compute effects.

**Weaken / downgrade:** Conventional tools recover the advantage, transfer fails, or interventions merely damage overall capability. Increased research automation alone never satisfies this threshold.

### Revision triggers

Audit or correction of runtime definitions; release of detailed methods or primary data; independent replication; contradictory quality-adjusted productivity evidence; causal model-lineage studies; or actual communication-representation experiments should trigger review. Update each dimension separately; none automatically upgrades EIC or IER.

## 7. 可检验预测与重新分级条件

**以下属于 IEH 推导的后续检验，不是已完成实验。**

### P1——从活动量到有效贡献

如果 Agent 参与扩大提升了研究生产率，在计入人类审查、失败实验及算力后，匹配项目应在单位总资源上产生更好的独立评定成果。

**升级条件：** 预注册或可信受控比较，并跨团队接受独立审计。

**削弱／降级条件：** 活动增加而有效成果停滞，或控制资源与选择因素后收益消失。这削弱生产率主张，不否定使用量增加。

### P2——从参与到因果改进闭环

追踪某项 Agent 产生的研究贡献进入后续模型；与未纳入该贡献的匹配版本比较；再检验模型改进是否提升下一轮研究效能。

**升级条件：** 在完整资源核算下，连续迭代出现可归因、可重复的增益。

**削弱／降级条件：** 贡献未被整合、改进不能迁移到研究，或人类／算力变化足以解释效果。即使受监督反馈闭环被验证，参与仍不等于自主递归自我改进。

### P3——先测通信结构，再判断通信语言

把 Agent 间任务相关通信与人类提示、工具调用、重复日志及共享存储读取分别测量。在预算、任务内容及协作要求匹配的条件下，比较人类可读通信与替代通信。

**增强通信假说的条件：** 学习形成的交换表征带来重复出现的因果效率收益，能迁移到陌生伙伴或任务，且普通协议不足以解释收益。

**削弱／降级条件：** 不存在语言瓶颈、表面收益只是序列化或压缩效应，或人类设计协议达到同等表现。仅有流量增加不满足检验。

### P4——HDCT 专属阈值

更强 HDCT 主张需要识别出具体认知工具，并证明稳定优势、迁移、重复外部验证，以及超出现有未经辅助人类操作或重建范围。内部表征干预应把该机制与一般算力效应分开。

**削弱／降级条件：** 常规工具能恢复优势、迁移失败，或干预只是损害一般能力。研究自动化增加本身永远不满足这一阈值。

### 修订触发条件

## 10. Methodological Relevance

1. Input volume is not outcome value.
2. Concurrency is not communication, and communication is not a new language.
3. AI-assisted AI development is not automatically autonomous self-improvement.
4. Distinguish source-reported measurements from independent audit and causal identification.
5. Preserve missing links as testable questions instead of filling them with an evolutionary narrative.

---

## 10. 方法论意义

1. 投入量不等于成果价值。
2. 并发不等于通信，通信不等于新语言。
3. AI 辅助 AI 研发不自动等于自主自我改进。
4. 区分来源报告的测量、独立审计与因果识别。
5. 把缺失环节保留为可检验问题，不以演化叙事填补证据。
