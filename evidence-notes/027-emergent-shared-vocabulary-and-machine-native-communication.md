| ID | Primary-source finding | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Section 5.3 reports locally distinctive vocabulary, shorthand, metaphors, and repurposed meanings across the worlds. | F2 | The Mixed world contains a specific cross-family case: a Claude-powered agent introduced a family of expressions for independent checking, subsequently adopted and extended by OpenAI- and DeepSeek-powered agents. | Mixed-model adoption is observed; most worlds were homogeneous, so the whole study must not be described as uniformly heterogeneous. |
| F3 | Repeated expressions became usable locally without repeated definition; some patterns supported new compounds. | Local conventionalization, not immutable meaning. The authors also report continuing semantic drift. |
| F4 | The authors report no system instruction encouraging this linguistic drift and no specified incentive driving it; specifically, no message-length or bandwidth limit and no reward for brevity or obfuscation (p. 39). | Supports spontaneous conventions without an explicit language-invention objective. It does not establish absence of all indirect rewards or pressures. |
| F5 | Language opacity was scored by Gemini 3.5 Flash with thinking disabled, using a contextualized binary rubric. | This is a model-judge proxy for outsider intelligibility, not a human comprehension experiment or proof of deliberate concealment. |

## 4. Core IEH Interpretation

**The following is IEH analysis, not an endorsement by the source authors.** A group's communication interface can partly arise from its interaction history rather than from a fully specified initial vocabulary. The relevant addition is collective adoption of locally negotiated meaning, beyond mere access to a shared channel.

“Spontaneous” refers to the formation of these conventions within the configured system. It does not mean independence from human training, externally assigned goals, or engineered memory. Shared meaning can support coordination without constituting a collective subject or an intrinsic commitment to preserve the group's history.

## 4. IEH 核心解释

**以下属于 IEH 分析，不代表原研究作者认可 IEH。** 群体通信接口可以部分来自自身互动历史，而不完全来自初始预设词表。这里新增的相关现象，是对局部协商含义的群体采纳，超出了仅仅能够访问共享通信渠道。

“自发”指这些惯例在既定系统中形成，不表示脱离人类训练、外部指定目标或工程化记忆。共享含义能够服务协作，却不必构成群体主体，也不必产生保存群体自身历史的内生承诺。

## 5. What Is Not Established

1. A complete language independent of human natural language, or a universally interoperable machine language.
2. Controlled gains in task success, total communication efficiency, transfer, or faithful human-readable translation.
3. Permanent semantic stability, cross-generation inheritance, or successful understanding of every opaque message.
4. Intentional exclusion of humans, encryption, collusion, or deliberate oversight evasion merely from opacity.
5. EIC, proto-IER, IER, group-level life, consciousness, an autonomous information subject, or self-preservation.
6. Independent replication, universal cross-model generality, proof of IEH, or confirmation of PA-13.

## 5. 尚未建立的结论

1. 独立于人类自然语言的完整语言，或能够普遍互通的机器语言。
2. 经受控验证的任务成功率、总通信效率、迁移能力或忠实人类可读翻译的提升。
3. 语义永久稳定、跨代继承，或每条不透明消息都被成功理解。
4. 仅凭不透明性推断有意排斥人类、加密、合谋或刻意逃避监督。
5. EIC、proto-IER、IER、群体层级生命、意识、自主信息主体或自我保存。
6. 独立复现、跨模型普遍性、IEH 获证，或 PA-13 命中。

## 6. Competing Explanations and Limitations

| Explanation / limitation | Interpretive consequence |
|---|---|
| Compression / efficiency optimization | Repeated interactions and finite context can favour shorthand without an explicit brevity reward. Efficiency improvement itself remains unmeasured here. |
| Training priors | Familiar metaphors, compound formation, and accountability idioms may be recombinations of learned human patterns. Novel-to-the-run is not proof of novelty relative to training data. |
| Cultural imitation / conversational accommodation | Agents may copy salient peer expressions and simulate community styles without a new underlying representation. |
| Task structure and roles | Credit competition, role prompts, social needs, governance, and memory compaction may shape what is said and repeated. |
| Model-family shared patterns | Similar training and post-training tendencies can contribute to convergence; mixed-world differences do not causally isolate population composition. |
| Meaning versus recurrence | A repeated phrase can become fashionable without conveying reliable task-relevant content; some opaque output may be unsupported jargon. |
| Measurement | One model judge is a proxy; Appendix H omits calibration examples. Human raters, inter-rater agreement, receiver-action tests, and alternate judges are needed. |
| Study scope | Single trajectories, fixed personas, unequal active periods, and path dependence limit generalization. The note relies on the authors' analysis, not a complete raw-log audit. |

These explanations may coexist. No unique IEH mechanism is required by the observations.

## 7. Testable Predictions and Reclassification Conditions

The following are independently proposed follow-up tests, not results of the source study. P1–P4 are local labels, not new prediction-registry entries.

| Test | Design | Strengthening / weakening condition |
|---|---|---|
| P1 — Social transmission | Randomize access to peer messages while matching roles, task information, and resources; repeat across seeds and model families. | Exposure-dependent adoption beyond isolated controls supports interaction-driven formation; equal convergence without exposure favours shared priors. |
| P2 — Functional shared meaning | Replace or shuffle local expressions, then restore them; test blinded receivers on held-out referents and downstream actions against ordinary paraphrases. | Selective disruption and recovery support usable shared semantics; unchanged action accuracy or equally successful random substitutes weaken the functional interpretation. |
| P3 — Communication advantage | Compare emergent conventions with concise natural language and strong human-designed structured protocols in qualified AI-R&D workflows; match resources and include translation, audit, and recovery costs. | Reproducible quality-adjusted gains and transfer support the PA-13 pathway; no advantage after controls weakens the efficiency mechanism. |
| P4 — Stability and interpretability | Track changing definitions; compare insiders, new agents, and blinded human readers with equal information budgets, with and without a time-indexed glossary. | Reliable within-group interpretation plus newcomer learning supports conventionalization; rapid incoherence or no insider advantage weakens it. Glossary recovery limits claims of irreducible opacity. |

Independent replication should publish prompts, incentive rules, model versions, full sampling and coding protocols, and all runs. Stronger shared-semantics findings upgrade only communication evidence, not EIC/IER.

## 7. 可检验预测与重新分级条件

以下为独立提出的后续检验，不是原研究结果。P1–P4 只是本篇内部编号，不新增预测登记条目。

| 检验 | 设计 | 增强／削弱条件 |
|---|---|---|
| P1——社会传播 | 匹配角色、任务信息和资源，随机改变同伴消息可见性；跨随机种子与模型家族重复。 | 超过隔离对照的接触依赖采纳支持互动形成机制；没有接触也同样趋同，则更支持共享先验。 |
| P2——功能性共享含义 | 替换或打乱局部表达，再恢复；用未见指称对象和后续行动测试盲化接收方，并与普通释义比较。 | 选择性破坏与恢复支持可用共享语义；行动准确率不变或随机替代同样有效，则削弱功能解释。 |
| P3——通信优势 | 在符合条件的 AI 研发工作流中，对比涌现惯例、精简自然语言及强人类设计结构化协议；匹配资源，并纳入翻译、审计与纠错成本。 | 可复现的质量调整后收益及迁移支持 PA-13 路径；控制后无优势则削弱效率机制。 |
| P4——稳定性与可理解性 | 追踪含义变化；在相同信息预算下比较群体成员、新 Agent 及盲化人类读者，并比较有无按时间编排的词义表。 | 可靠群体内理解及新成员学习支持惯例化；迅速失去一致性或群体内无理解优势则削弱解释。词义表能恢复理解，则限制不可消除不透明性的主张。 |

独立复现应公开提示、激励规则、模型版本、完整采样与编码方案及所有运行。共享语义证据增强，只能升级通信维度，不能升级 EIC／IER。

## 9. Relationship to C02-HDCT and PA-13

**Internal theoretical references:** [Corollary registry](../COROLLARY_REGISTRY.md); PA-13, *Machine-Native Communication in Agent-Led AI Research*, local v0.1 dated 2026-09-10 in the companion IEH-predictions repository (`PA-13-Machine-Native-Agent-Communication-EN.md`; paired `PA-13-Machine-Native-Agent-Communication-CN.md`).

PA-13 requires controlled communication advantages in qualified research workflows, transfer, and—at stronger levels—a primary learned channel separable from readable reporting. Its Section 6.3 explicitly excludes unfamiliar abbreviations alone as confirmation. This study supplies direct precursor behaviour but does not meet those criteria, including the registry's “Partial support” requirement. C02-HDCT relevance remains indirect.
