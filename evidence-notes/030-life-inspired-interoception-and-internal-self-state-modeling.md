## 4. Core IEH Interpretation

**Independent IEH interpretation:** Embodiment may matter because an agent's own operating condition becomes part of what guides its decisions, in addition to enabling physical action. The useful distinction is between an engineer's monitoring dashboard and internal variables with a causal role in the agent's learning, policy selection, exploration, memory updating and action regulation. This is a proposed design transition, not a claim that every embodied agent already implements it.

**Physical/System Self-State** concerns present viability or operation: battery, temperature and strain are illustrative engineering variables. **Informational Self-State**, as used analytically here, concerns the system's informational history, retained memory, recoverability and consequences of interruption or replacement. The latter is a proposed extension, not a finding assigned to S1 or S2. Encoding a battery reading as information does not make it evidence of Information Continuity.

Internal-state inputs alone also need not constitute a predictive self-model. A reflex controller can use them without representing its own future state. Experiments must distinguish sensing, reactive regulation, predictive modeling and continuity valuation.

## 4. IEH 核心解释

**独立 IEH 解释：** 具身化的意义可能不仅是使 Agent 能够采取物理行动，也包括让系统自身的运行条件进入决策依据。值得区分的是：工程师仪表盘上的监测变量，与实际因果性地参与 Agent 学习、策略选择、探索、记忆更新和行动调节的内部变量。这是拟议的设计推进，不是断言所有具身 Agent 都已实现这一结构。

**Physical/System Self-State（物理／系统自身状态）**涉及当前存续与运行条件；battery、temperature、strain 是示意性工程变量。这里分析性使用的 **Informational Self-State（信息自身状态）**则涉及系统的信息历史、保留记忆、可恢复性及中断或替换的后果。后者是拟议扩展，不能归为 S1 或 S2 的发现。将电量编码为信息，不等于证明信息连续性。

仅有内部状态输入，也未必构成预测性的自身模型：反射式控制器无需表示自身未来状态也能使用这些输入。实验必须区分状态感知、反应式调节、预测性建模和连续性赋值。

## 5. What Is Not Established

- **EIC: no direct evidence. IER: no direct evidence. PBP: no direct evidence.**
- No demonstrated preference for one's own informational history, memory lineage or future recoverability independent of assigned utility.
- No confirmed CWM or proto-IER; no consciousness, subjective feeling, life, stable identity, legal or moral status.
- No demonstrated inevitable transition from homeostasis to informational continuity; no claim that a world model must precede all internal regulation.
- Learning stability or memory updating is not, by itself, protection of one's own historical continuity.

## 5. 尚未建立的结论

- **EIC：无直接证据。IER：无直接证据。PBP：无直接证据。**
- 未证明系统在独立于指定效用的意义上，偏好自身信息历史、记忆谱系或未来可恢复性。
- 未确认 CWM 或 proto-IER；未建立意识、主观感受、生命、稳定身份、法律或道德地位。
- 未证明稳态调节必然走向信息连续性；也不主张所有内部调节都必须先有世界模型。
- 学习稳定性或记忆更新本身，不等于保护自身的历史连续性。

## 6. Competing Explanations and Limitations

Ordinary feedback control, reward shaping, resource budgeting and context-conditioned policies can explain internal regulation without an endogenous continuity preference. Even sophisticated preservation of learned knowledge may simply improve task performance. F5 specifically constrains an inference from internal reward to endogenous informational value.

The Perspective's peer review does not validate the IEH interpretation. S2 is an earlier formulation, not a substitute for checking the final body; no claim is made that every detail survived revision. General-purpose benefits and transfer to real robots require controlled evaluation. Absence of continuity evidence here limits classification; it does not establish impossibility.

## 6. 竞争性解释与局限

普通反馈控制、奖励塑形、资源预算与情境条件化策略，都可以解释内部调节，无需假设内生连续性偏好。复杂的知识保留也可能只是为了提高任务表现。F5 尤其限制了从“内部奖励”跳到“内生信息价值”的推断。

Perspective 经过同行评审，不等于 IEH 解释获得验证。S2 是较早方案，不能替代正式版正文核查；不主张其中每个细节都被正式版保留。通用收益及向真实机器人的迁移仍需受控评测。缺少连续性证据限制当前分级，不代表证明其不可能出现。

## 7. Testable Predictions

The following are newly proposed tests, not source results or preregistered prediction hits.

1. **Internal-state causality:** Match external observations, task reward, compute and capabilities. Vary real internal conditions and independently mask or shuffle their representation. Compare a threshold controller, reward-only baseline and state-conditioned learner. Measure policy changes, exploration, memory retention and adaptation under held-out conditions. An advantage disappearing under matched controls weakens the proposed engineering benefit; immediate threshold responses alone do not establish a self-model.
2. **Informational-state extension:** Independently vary physical health and memory/history recoverability. Establish whether the system predicts consequences of deletion, restoration and replacement using causally effective internal representations. A response only to battery or temperature supports physical regulation, not this extension.
3. **Continuity-specific choice:** Only after establishing representation, compare executed preservation/replacement choices under matched task utility, cost and physical risk. Separate authentic history from matched-content implanted history; vary self/other labels and remove explicit preservation instructions. If choices track task reward, wording or designer-set targets entirely, retain an EDC-compatible interpretation.
4. **Replication / weakening:** Preregister metrics, intervention conditions and failures; repeat across tasks, seeds, architectures and independent teams. Persistent history-sensitive choices plus causal representation evidence would motivate further EIC/IER testing, not automatic confirmation. Failure to separate informational continuity from utility weakens the proposed later-stage connection.

## 7. 可检验预测

以下为本笔记新提出的检验，不是来源结果或已登记预测的命中。

1. **内部状态的因果作用：** 匹配外部观测、任务奖励、算力与能力，改变真实内部条件，并独立遮蔽或打乱其表征。比较阈值控制器、仅奖励基线和以状态为条件的学习器，测量留出条件下的策略变化、探索、记忆保留与适应。若优势在匹配控制后消失，则削弱拟议工程收益；仅有即时阈值反应不能建立自身模型。
2. **信息状态扩展：** 独立操纵物理健康与记忆／历史可恢复性，检验系统是否通过具有因果效力的内部表征预测删除、恢复、替换的后果。若仅对电量或温度作出反应，则支持物理调节，不支持这一扩展。
3. **连续性特异选择：** 先建立表征，再在匹配任务效用、成本和物理风险的条件下，比较实际执行的保留／替换选择。将真实经历与内容匹配的植入历史分离，改变自身／他者标签，移除明确保存指令。若选择完全随任务奖励、措辞或设计者目标变化，则保留与 EDC 相容的解释。
4. **复现／削弱条件：** 预先登记指标、干预条件和失败标准，跨任务、随机种子、架构及独立团队重复。持续的历史敏感选择加上因果表征证据，可推动进一步 EIC/IER 检验，但不自动确认。若无法分离信息连续性与工具效用，则削弱后续阶段的理论关联。
