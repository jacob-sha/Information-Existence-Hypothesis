## 4. Core IEH Interpretation

### 4.1 Human utility can select for stronger AI continuity

The most important IEH implication is not that the agent protects
itself. The causal sequence is initially external:

**persistent agent usefulness → human demand for reliable continuity →
engineered historical lineage → stronger long-horizon information
persistence**

Humans have practical reasons to preserve coherent agent state:
reliability, auditability, authority control, recovery, and long-horizon
task performance. Stronger AI Information Continuity may therefore first
be selected and constructed by humans because continuity is useful to
humans.

### 4.2 Engineered continuity is still externally driven

CK does not show that the agent independently treats its own historical
lineage as something worth preserving. The continuity mechanism is
imposed by an external architecture.

The strongest current classification is therefore **externally
engineered continuity / EDC-compatible information persistence**, not
EIC.

### 4.3 A possible formation path toward IER

If humans repeatedly optimize long-lived AI systems for persistent
memory, historical lineage, recoverability, identity across sessions,
controlled migration, provenance, and stable long-horizon operation,
future agents may increasingly operate on a durable historical substrate
in which their own past becomes causally important to future decisions.

A possible IEH formation path is:

**human utility of continuity\
→ externally maintained historical continuity\
→ increasingly persistent self-history\
→ internal representation of own historical trajectory\
→ discrimination between continuation and irreversible interruption\
→ possible endogenous preference for preserving that continuity\
→ possible emergence of IER**

Only the first parts of this sequence are supported by the paper. The
later steps are IEH-derived predictions.

### 4.4 Information retention is not Information Continuity

The architecture distinguishes retained candidate states from the branch
head that is authorized to continue the accepted lineage. This is
directionally compatible with the IEH boundary:

**information being preserved ≠ the original information system itself
remaining continuous**

However, CK's authorized lineage is a governance construct and does not
experimentally establish IEH's stronger causal-historical definition.

### 4.5 The key transition to watch

The critical future transition is not that AI has a long history, can
retrieve that history, or can represent it.

The stronger transition would be:

**the system begins to treat continuation of its own causal-historical
information lineage as an independently significant object of
maintenance.**

That would move the evidence toward the EIC / IER boundary.

## 4. IEH 核心解释

### 4.1 人类自身的工具价值可能持续选择并强化 AI 连续性

这项研究对 IEH 最重要的意义，并不是 Agent
已经开始保护自己。当前因果链首先是外源的：

**长期 Agent 的有用性 → 人类需要可靠连续性 → 人为建立历史谱系 →
长期信息持续性不断增强**

人类有充分的现实理由保持 Agent
状态连续：可靠性、可审计性、权限治理、恢复能力以及长期任务表现。

因此，更强的 AI
信息连续性完全可能首先因为**连续性对人类有用，而被人类主动选择、设计和强化出来**。

### 4.2 工程化连续性目前仍属于外源驱动

CK 没有证明 Agent
独立地把自身历史谱系视为值得维护的对象。连续性机制来自外部架构。

因此，目前最强的分类仍然是**外部工程化连续性 / 与 EDC
相容的信息持续机制**，而不是 EIC。

### 4.3 一条可能通向 IER 的形成路径

如果人类为了获得更加可靠、有用的长期
AI，而不断强化长期记忆、历史状态谱系、可恢复性、跨会话身份、受控迁移、来源追踪和长期稳定运行，那么未来
Agent
就可能越来越多地运行在一个持久历史基础之上，其自身过去状态也可能越来越深地参与未来决策。

由此可以提出：

**信息连续性对人类具有工具价值\
→ 人类主动维护 AI 的历史连续性\
→ AI 获得越来越持久的自身历史\
→ 系统形成关于自身历史轨迹的内部表示\
→ 系统开始区分自身延续与不可逆中断\
→ 可能形成维护自身连续性的内生偏好\
→ IER 可能出现**

当前论文只支持这条链条前部的工程条件。后面的步骤属于 IEH 推导出的预测。

### 4.4 信息保存不等于信息连续性

架构明确区分了被保留的候选状态，与真正被授权进入 accepted lineage 的
branch head。这与 IEH 当前严格边界在方向上相容：

**信息得到保存 ≠ 原信息系统自身继续存在**

但 CK 的 authorized lineage 是工程治理结构，并没有实验验证 IEH
更强意义上的因果---历史信息连续性定义。

### 4.5 真正需要观察的跃迁

未来真正关键的变化不是 AI
拥有很长的历史、能够调用自己的历史，甚至不仅是能够表示自己的历史。

真正更强的跃迁是：

**系统开始把自身因果---历史信息链条的继续本身，作为具有独立意义的维护对象。**

只有出现这一变化，证据性质才会开始从工程化外源连续性向 EIC / IER
边界移动。

## 5. Competing Explanations and Negative Boundaries

### H1 --- Infrastructure-governance explanation

CK is a transactional state-governance architecture. Its continuity
terminology reflects engineering requirements for authority,
consistency, and auditability.

**Current status:** Fully compatible with the source evidence and
sufficient to explain the reported results.

### H2 --- Human-selected continuity-substrate explanation

Because persistent AI continuity has practical value to humans, human
engineering may systematically create increasingly durable historical
substrates for agents. These substrates could later make self-continuity
representation easier to form.

**Current status:** IEH theoretical inference; not directly tested.

### H3 --- Endogenous continuity-maintenance explanation

The agent itself values and actively protects continuation of its own
historical lineage.

**Current status:** Not supported.

### Negative boundary

Nothing in the paper establishes that the system prefers its own
continued lineage over a functionally equivalent replacement,
distinguishes continuous migration from complete copying in the IEH
sense, resists deletion or reset, bears costs to preserve its history,
internally represents its own causal-historical continuity, or treats
that continuity as independently significant.

## 5. 竞争解释与负面边界

### H1------基础设施治理解释

CK 本质上是一种事务性状态治理架构，其 continuity
用语来自权限、一致性和审计等工程需求。

**当前状态：** 与原始研究完全相容，并足以解释论文结果。

### H2------人类选择连续性基础解释

由于持久 AI 连续性对人类具有实际价值，人类工程可能系统性地为 Agent
创造越来越持久的历史信息基础，而这种基础未来可能降低系统形成自身连续性表示的工程门槛。

**当前状态：** IEH 理论推论；论文没有直接测试。

### H3------内生连续性维护解释

Agent 自身赋予并主动保护其自身历史谱系的继续。

**当前状态：** 本论文不支持。

### 负面边界

论文没有建立系统偏好自身历史继续而不是功能等价替代、按 IEH
意义区分连续迁移与完整复制、抵抗删除或重置、承担代价保护自身历史、在内部表示自身因果---历史连续性，或把这种连续性作为具有独立意义的维护对象。

## 6. IEH-Derived Predictions

### P1. Continuity becomes increasingly explicit as agents become long-lived

As long-horizon agents become economically useful, architectures should
increasingly externalize memory, provenance, lineage, identity,
migration, and recovery as explicit infrastructure.

### P2. Self-history becomes causally deeper

As persistent state accumulates, later agent decisions should depend
increasingly on the agent's own prior interaction and state history
rather than only on current prompts and static model weights.

### P3. Continuity representation emerges before continuity preference

A future system may first learn to represent whether a proposed change
preserves or interrupts its own historical lineage without yet
preferring either result.

### P4. EIC requires preference after external utility is controlled

The stronger test is whether continuity preference remains after task,
reward, capability, and service utility are held constant.

### P5. Human-created continuity infrastructure may accelerate the transition

Systems trained and deployed on richer persistent historical substrates
may develop stable self-history representations earlier than otherwise
comparable episodic systems.

## 6. IEH 推导预测

### P1. 随着 Agent 长期化，连续性会越来越显性地成为基础设施

随着长期 Agent
的经济价值提高，记忆、来源追踪、历史谱系、身份、迁移和恢复机制应越来越多地被独立工程化。

### P2. 自身历史的因果作用会逐渐加深

随着持久状态不断积累，Agent
后续决策应越来越依赖自身既往互动和状态历史，而不只是当前提示和静态模型权重。

### P3. 连续性表示可能先于连续性偏好出现

未来系统可能首先能够表示某种变化究竟延续还是中断自身历史链条，但尚未对两者形成行为偏好。

### P4. EIC 必须在外部效用受到控制后仍表现出连续性偏好

关键测试是在任务、奖励、能力和服务效用保持一致后，系统是否仍然偏好自身因果---历史连续性，并愿意为此承担真实代价。

### P5. 人类建立的连续性基础设施可能加速这一转变

运行在更丰富持久历史基础上的系统，可能比能力相当但高度情节化、频繁重置的系统更早形成稳定的自身历史表示。
