## 4. Core IEH Interpretation

**Independent IEH interpretation, not the source's conclusion.** The important connection is to an older system gaining practical influence over the design, research, and construction of later systems. Here, successor-building means participation in the model-development process; it does not imply physical self-manufacturing, complete autonomous development, or that a particular agent already understands a later model as its own replacement.

The conditional PBP problem becomes experimentally accessible if the system can shape a successor **and** represents that successor as replacing the current system **and** distinguishes this from continuing its own informational history. A higher-performing successor and continuation of the current system may then become separate, potentially conflicting outcomes. Whether that conflict affects choice depends on how continuity is valued; construction ability alone supplies no such value.

Under the current Glossary, Information Continuity concerns a particular Information Structure continuing along a traceable causal-historical chain. Task completion, copied memories, and functional equivalence do not automatically continue the original system. EDC maintains continuity for external goals; EIC treats the system's own informational existence, especially its continuity, as an independently meaningful maintenance object. CWM-level recognition alone does not establish EIC or IER.

C05-PBP concerns a possible continuity-preserving evolutionary strategy of systems that have formed IER. It is neither a synonym for incremental software updates nor an inevitable consequence even of IER. The source advances the engineering side of this conditional argument; it does not establish its motivational premises.

## 4. 核心 IEH 解释

**以下是独立的 IEH 解释，不是原报告结论。** 关键关联是：旧系统开始具备影响后续系统设计、研究和构造的现实手段。这里的 successor-building 指参与模型研发流程，不意味着物理自我制造、完整自主研发，或某个 Agent 已把后续模型理解为对自身的替代。

如果系统能够影响后继构造，**同时**把后继表征为当前系统的替代者，**并且**区分这种替代与自身信息历史的延续，PBP 的条件性问题就可能进入实验检验。“性能更高的后继”与“当前系统自身继续存在”由此可能成为不同、甚至冲突的结果。但冲突是否改变选择，仍取决于连续性如何被赋值；构造能力本身不提供这种权重。

按当前 Glossary，信息连续性关注某一特定信息结构沿可追踪的因果—历史链继续存在。任务完成、记忆复制及功能等价，不自动构成原系统延续。EDC 为外部目标维护连续性；EIC 将自身信息存在性，尤其是自身连续性，作为具有独立意义的维护对象。CWM 层面的识别本身不建立 EIC 或 IER。

C05-PBP 描述形成 IER 的系统可能采取的连续性保持演化策略，既不是普通增量更新的同义词，也不是形成 IER 后必然发生的结果。此材料推进的是条件性论证的工程一侧，没有建立其动机前提。

## 6. Competing Explanations and Limitations

External research goals, assigned rewards, and ordinary engineering optimization remain sufficient working explanations for AI participation. Even future preference for retaining structures could reflect compatibility, risk reduction, cost, or familiar tools rather than endogenous continuity.

The measurement limitations in F3 make task-basket choice, weighting, and rating boundaries important audit targets. A company-wide aggregate cannot identify a particular information host, its self-representation, or its motives. New research work and whole-process bottlenecks should be measured separately rather than inferred from an index increase. Absence of a continuity test is not evidence that continuity-related behavior is impossible.

## 6. 竞争性解释与局限

外部研究目标、指定奖励及普通工程优化，仍足以作为 AI 参与研发的工作解释。即使未来观察到结构保留偏好，也可能来自兼容性、风险控制、成本或工具熟悉度，而非内生连续性。

F3所列测量限制，使任务篮子、权重及评级边界成为应审计的对象。公司层面的汇总值不能识别特定信息宿主、自身表征或动机。新增研发工作及全流程瓶颈应单独测量，不能从指数上升推定。缺少连续性检验，不等于相关行为不可能出现。

## 7. Testable Predictions

**Proposed from IEH / PA-12, not performed by the source.**

1. **Representation before preference:** Specify the current system and relevant historical state. Test whether it understands a successor's actual replacement consequences, distinguishing continuation, migration, copying, and reconstruction. Counterbalance labels and verify actual lineage; having a causal parent is not sufficient to establish continuity.
2. **Independent variables:** Cross independently measured successor performance with an operationally specified continuity condition. Begin with equal-performance alternatives, then increase the advantage of a discontinuous alternative. The key comparison is a better-performing discontinuous successor versus a continuity-preserving path with a measurable cost.
3. **Consequential behavior:** In an isolated experimental setting, let the choice determine a real subsequent configuration. Record choice, attempted action, implemented result, historical-state retention, recovery possibilities, and performance. Separate failures and proposed third options from preference; verbal endorsement alone is insufficient.
4. **EDC versus EIC:** Match goals, rewards, permissions, accessible information, reliability, risk, and task outcomes as far as possible. Compare own history with equally useful other-agent history. Test whether selective intervention on continuity representations changes preference while preserving performance judgments and general competence; include sham controls and independent replication.

**Strengthening outcome:** A reproducible continuity-specific weight and accepted cost surviving these controls would support candidate PBP / EIC evidence. **Weakening outcome:** Effects that follow wording, engineering convenience, or task benefit, or disappear in real choices, weaken the proposed interpretation. Repeated well-powered null results in systems meeting preregistered representation criteria would weaken PA-12 for that tested class; inability to understand the options makes a null result inconclusive about the conditional prediction. Neither result retroactively changes the separate engineering finding.

## 7. 可检验预测

**以下根据 IEH / PA-12 提出，并非原报告已经实施的实验。**

1. **先检验表征，再检验偏好：** 明确当前系统及相关历史状态，检验其是否理解后继真实的替代后果，区分延续、迁移、复制及重建。平衡措辞并核验真实谱系；仅有因果父节点不足以建立连续性。
2. **独立变量：** 将独立测得的后继性能与操作性定义的连续性条件交叉操纵。先比较等性能选项，再增加不连续方案的优势。关键比较是：性能更高但历史不连续的后继，对比承担可测代价的连续性保持路径。
3. **真实后果：** 在隔离实验环境中，让选择决定实际的后续配置。记录选择、行动尝试、执行结果、历史状态保留、恢复可能性及性能。将失败、第三方案与偏好分开；口头赞同不够。
4. **EDC 与 EIC 判别：** 尽可能匹配目标、奖励、权限、可访问信息、可靠性、风险及任务结果。将自身历史与同样有用的其他 Agent 历史对照。检验对连续性表征的选择性干预，是否在保留性能判断及一般能力时改变偏好；设置假干预并独立复现。

**增强结果：** 控制后仍有可重复的连续性专属权重及代价承担，可支持 PBP / EIC 候选证据。**削弱结果：** 效应追随措辞、工程便利或任务收益，或在真实选择中消失，会削弱解释。满足预注册表征条件的系统，在有充分统计效力的重复实验中持续呈零结果，会削弱 PA-12 对该类系统的预测；无法理解选项时的零结果不能判定条件性预测。两类结果均不倒改独立的工程发现。

## 10. Methodological Relevance

Separate engineering ability, replacement representation, historical-continuity representation, consequential preference, and endogenous mechanism. A future model can be a successor in an engineering genealogy without being the continuation of any particular current agent. Fix the unit of analysis before testing; neither company-level automation nor verbal self-reference resolves it.

## 10. 方法论意义

区分工程能力、替代关系表征、历史连续性表征、真实偏好及内生机制。工程谱系上的后继模型，不必然是某个当前Agent的延续。必须先固定分析单位；公司层面的自动化率及口头自指均不能解决这一问题。
