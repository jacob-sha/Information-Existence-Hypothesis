# Changelog

## IEH v1.2 — IER Test and Instrumental Self-Preservation Distinction

Released: 2026-07-13

### Theory revisions

- Distinguished task-driven instrumental self-preservation from Information Existence Right (IER).
- Expanded the Information Existence Right Test (IER Test) as a multidimensional, nonbinary assessment framework.
- Distinguished Task Continuity, Functional Continuity, a system’s own Information Continuity, and Subject-Information Historical Continuity.
- Revised Corollaries I, IV, V, VII, VIII, IX, X, and XI accordingly.
- Clarified that highly autonomous operation and Informational Resilience are capability dimensions rather than sufficient evidence of IER.
- Revised Autonomy of Silicon-based Intelligence (ASI) to require the combination of IER and physical self-maintenance.
- Reframed the Silicon Cambrian as a conditional probability driven by environmental divergence, communication constraints, and local adaptation.
- Reframed AI Alignment as IER-based coordination of Information Continuity boundaries under the priority of human safety.

### Repository revisions

- Synchronized Chinese and English chapter collections with the root full-text editions.
- Updated the terminology standard, theory map, corollary registry, figure index, prediction index, and evidence note.
- Added repository build and consistency-check scripts.
- Updated citation metadata and version records.
