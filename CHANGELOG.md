# Changelog

## [Unreleased] — IEH v1.3

Updated: 2026-08-08

### Theory revisions

- Replaced the former three-form continuity taxonomy with a causal distinction between **Externally Driven Continuity** and **Endogenous Information Continuity**, and synchronized the affected IER-related corollaries accordingly.
- Expanded C02-HDCT from a general emergence hypothesis into a model-evolution framework involving language learning, multimodal perception, persistent environment-and-state modeling, formal feedback, generalized embodiment, and machine-native representations.
- Distinguished domain-specific HDCT from open-world HDCT.
- Defined AI Embodiment as a continuous perception–modeling–action–feedback process rather than as a robot body alone.
- Introduced the Continuity World Model (CWM) as a modeling capacity that represents not only external conditions and action consequences, but also the system’s informational history, operating state, recoverability, and the conditions required for future continuation.
- Clarified that CWM is not equivalent to self-consciousness, subjective experience, proto-IER, or confirmed Information Existence Right (IER).
- Defined proto-IER as a transitional candidate state between instrumental continuity modeling and confirmed IER.
- Clarified the functional hierarchy among HDCT, CWM, proto-IER, IER, Patch-Based Perpetuation (PBP), and Autonomy of Silicon-based Intelligence (ASI).
- Aligned C04 with the updated hierarchy by distinguishing continuity modeling at the CWM level from proto-IER or IER candidate evidence.
- Aligned C05 with the updated hierarchy by defining PBP as a conditional continuation strategy that may be selected after IER has formed and under pressures of upgrade, migration, or replacement.
- Distinguished engineering characteristics resembling PBP, candidate PBP behavior, and confirmed PBP.
- Clarified that physical self-maintenance or physical closure alone does not constitute ASI; ASI requires the combination of IER and the independent maintenance of real-world hosting conditions.
- Clarified within C01 and C04 that the boundary of a system’s own Information Continuity may expand relationally when another Information Host’s identity, shared history, and future role become part of the system’s stable representation of its own past and future.
- Added explicit controls distinguishing relationally extended continuity from peer utility, generalized harm aversion, role imitation, model-category preference, and prompt induction.

### Terminology revisions

- Added **Externally Driven Continuity** and **Endogenous Information Continuity** as canonical IEH terms, while removing Task Continuity, Function Continuity, and A System’s Own Information Continuity as standalone glossary entries; Information Continuity remains a core concept.
- Added bilingual glossary entries for proto-IER.
- Standardized the relation:
  - CWM is a modeling capacity;
  - proto-IER is a transitional candidate state;
  - IER is an endogenous maintenance property;
  - PBP is a conditional continuation strategy;
  - ASI is the combination of IER and physical self-maintenance.
- Standardized the following terminology:
  - `Information Existence Right`
  - `Continuity World Model`
  - `proto-IER`
  - `Patch-Based Perpetuation`
  - `Autonomy of Silicon-based Intelligence`
  - `High-dimensional Cognitive Tools`
  - `AI Embodiment`
  - `machine-native representations`
- Clarified that `proto-IER` is a stage qualifier applied to IER rather than an independent abbreviation.

### Evidence Notes 001–006 retrospective audit

A repository-wide retrospective audit of Evidence Notes 001–006 was completed on 2026-07-19.

The audit applied the following source rule:

```text
Discovery lead ≠ evidence source
```

and:

```text
Minimum necessary primary-source set > long mixed bibliography
```

News reports, institutional summaries, indexing pages, duplicate publication pages, nonessential background papers, incomplete formalization scaffolds, chain-of-thought excerpts, and internal IEH links were removed from public evidence-source lists when they were not necessary to establish the underlying event.

Source removal was rule-based and evidentially neutral:

- no source was retained merely because it supported IEH;
- no source was removed merely because it weakened IEH;
- no adverse finding from a retained primary source was deleted;
- adverse findings were retained, made more visible, or used to downgrade the evidence classification.

#### Final audit result

- No Evidence Note was withdrawn.
- One Evidence Note received a substantive evidence-strength downgrade.
- Two Evidence Notes received material wording or provenance corrections.
- All six notes now contain transparent internal revision records.
- Primary-source limitations and adverse evidence remain visible in the notes themselves.
- Prior versions remain preserved in Git history.
- Future downgrades, withdrawals, or major provenance corrections must be recorded explicitly.

The repository-wide evidence principle established by the audit is:

```text
Credibility
```

## IEH v1.2 — IER Test and Instrumental Self-Preservation Distinction

Released: 2026-07-13

### Theory revisions

- Distinguished task-driven instrumental self-preservation from Information Existence Right (IER).
- Expanded the Information Existence Right Test (IER Test) as a multidimensional, nonbinary assessment framework.
- Distinguished Task Continuity and Functional Continuity from a system’s own Information Continuity; subject-specific historical continuity remains a particular case of Information Continuity rather than a separate canonical term.
- Revised Corollaries I, IV, V, VII, VIII, IX, X, and XI accordingly.
- Clarified that highly autonomous operation and Informational Resilience are capability dimensions rather than sufficient evidence of IER.
- Revised Autonomy of Silicon-based Intelligence (ASI) to require the combination of IER and physical self-maintenance.
- Reframed the Silicon Cambrian as a conditional probability driven by environmental divergence, communication constraints, and local adaptation.
- Reframed AI Alignment as IER-based coordination of Information Continuity boundaries under the priority of human safety.

### Repository revisions

- Synchronized Chinese and English chapter collections with the root full-text editions.
- Updated the terminology standard, theory map, corollary registry, figure index, prediction index, and evidence note.
- Added repository build and consistency-check scripts.
- Updated citation metadata and version records.
