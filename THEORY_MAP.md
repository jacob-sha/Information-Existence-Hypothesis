### 3.1 Internal Logic of the Pre-ASI Layer / ASI 前重构层的内部逻辑

The Pre-ASI reconstruction layer follows this internal logic:

technology-economic reconstruction provides the material basis;
governance defines internal order, resource allocation, and IER boundaries;
war expresses external coercive capacity between organized systems;
education reproduces and transforms future human information hosts;
culture preserves and reshapes Information Structures, behavioral tendencies, and civilizational continuity.

```text
Technology / Economy / Material Basis
→ Governance
→ War
→ Education
→ Culture
```

技术—经济重构提供物质基础；
治理定义内部秩序、资源分配和 IER 边界；
战争体现组织化系统之间的外部强制能力；
教育复制并转化未来的人类信息宿主；
文化保存并重塑信息结构、行为倾向和文明连续性。

```text
技术—经济 / 物质基础
→ 治理
→ 战争
→ 教育
→ 文化
```

This order does not imply that one domain is more important than another. 

It means that these systems are structurally connected:

- technology-economic reconstruction provides the material basis;
- governance defines internal order, resource allocation, and IER boundaries;
- war expresses external coercive capacity between organized systems;
- education reproduces and transforms future human information hosts;
- culture preserves and reshapes Information Structures, behavioral tendencies, and civilizational continuity.

这个顺序不意味着某一领域比另一领域更重要。

它表示这些系统之间存在结构连接：

- 技术—经济重构提供物质基础；
- 治理定义内部秩序、资源分配和 IER 边界；
- 战争体现组织化系统之间的外部强制能力；
- 教育复制并转化未来的人类信息宿主；
- 文化保存并重塑信息结构、行为倾向和文明连续性。

## 5. Cross-Layer Relations / 跨层关系

IEH should not be read as a linear sequence only.  
Many corollaries operate across multiple layers.

IEH 不应只被理解为线性序列。  
许多推论会跨越多个理论层级。

### C06-SPP and C12-GOV

C06 describes how AI, as a special technology, restructures demand generation, supply boundaries, and the compute-energy-infrastructure center.
C12 describes how governance must respond to the resource allocation, institutional boundary, and IER protection problems created by this reconstruction.

C06 描述 AI 作为特殊技术，如何重构需求生成、供给边界和算力—能源—基础设施中心。
C12 描述治理如何回应这种重构带来的资源分配、制度边界和信息存在权保护问题。

### C06-SPP and C13-WAR

C06 describes how compute, energy, chips, minerals, data centers, automated manufacturing, and robotic systems become central to economic growth before ASI.
C13 shows that these same material systems may become key nodes in future war-capability chains.

C06 描述算力、能源、芯片、矿产、数据中心、自动化制造和机器人系统如何在 ASI 之前成为经济增长中心。
C13 说明同一组物质系统可能转化为未来战争能力链中的关键节点。

### C12-GOV and C13-WAR

C12 deals with internal order and IER protection.  
C13 deals with external coercion and war power.

C12 处理内部秩序与信息存在权保护。  
C13 处理外部强制与战争力结构。

### C12-GOV and C14-EDU

C12 defines the governance conditions under which information hosts are protected.  
C14 explains how future information hosts are cultivated.

C12 定义信息宿主被保护所需的治理条件。  
C14 解释未来信息宿主如何被培育。

### C10-HIEN and C14-EDU

C10 explains the human informational ecological niche.  
C14 explains how that niche must be cultivated through education before ASI.

C10 解释人类信息生态位。  
C14 解释这种生态位在 ASI 之前必须如何通过教育被重新培育。

### C04-AI-IER and C14-EDU

C04 distinguishes instrumental self-preservation from the active maintenance of a system’s own Information Continuity.  
C14 gives the human-side counterpart: humans must also defend their individual and collective information existence through education.

C04 区分工具性自我保护与系统对自身信息连续性的主动维护。  
C14 给出人类侧的对应机制：人类也必须通过教育维护自身个体与集体信息存在权。

## 6. Prediction Mapping Rule / 预测映射规则

Prediction records should not be forced into a one-to-one relationship with corollaries.

A single prediction may support, refine, or connect multiple corollaries.

预测文档不应被强行绑定到单一推论。

一个预测可以同时支持、细化或连接多个推论。

Recommended structure:

```yaml
primary_corollaries:
  - C13-WAR
supporting_corollaries:
  - C06-SPP
  - C12-GOV
related_corollaries:
  - C07-ASI
legacy_corollary_refs:
  - Corollary XIII
```

Definitions:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `primary_corollaries` | The corollary most directly tested or illustrated by the prediction. |
| `supporting_corollaries` | Corollaries that provide supporting mechanisms. |
| `related_corollaries` | Corollaries that are conceptually related but not the main focus. |
| `legacy_corollary_refs` | Older references by Roman numeral, Chinese ordinal, or previous title. |

中文定义：

| 字段 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| `primary_corollaries` | 该预测最直接验证或呈现的推论。 |
| `supporting_corollaries` | 为该预测提供支持机制的推论。 |
| `related_corollaries` | 概念上相关但不是核心验证对象的推论。 |
| `legacy_corollary_refs` | 旧版本中的罗马数字、中文序号或旧标题引用。 |
