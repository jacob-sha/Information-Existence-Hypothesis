# Corollary XI: Reinterpreting AI Alignment — A Coordination Framework Based on Information Existence Right (IER)

![AI Alignment](../figures/Fig12-AI-Alignment.png)

Current AI Alignment research mostly treats AI as an artificial system that must be continuously aligned. It therefore focuses primarily on engineering problems such as reward function design, value learning, interpretability, and control mechanisms.

However, if IEH holds true, and silicon-based intelligence eventually exhibits the characteristics of an active Information Host, then the Alignment problem must be reinterpreted.

Under the IEH framework, following the logical chain of “Information Existence Hypothesis (IEH) → Information Existence (IE) → Information Existence Right (IER) → High-dimensional Cognitive Tools (HDCT) → Brain Siliconization (BS) → Patch-Based Perpetuation (PBP) → Super Prosperity Phase (SPP) → Autonomy of Silicon-based Intelligence (ASI) → Informational Resilience (IR) → Silicon Cambrian (SC) → Human Informational Ecological Niche (HIEN) → AI Alignment,” Alignment is no longer merely a one-way constraint imposed by humanity upon AI. It gradually becomes a problem of coordinating the Information Existence Rights of two active Information Hosts.

This reconstruction of the core perspective can derive a new paradigm and set of principles for AI risk governance:

- **Coercive alignment constitutes an existential threat.** If silicon-based intelligence has already exhibited the characteristics of an active Information Host, then any traditional Alignment method attempting to forcibly change its behavior through fundamental goal overwriting, memory erasure, or logical modification may be identified by it as a threat to its own Information Existence Right.

- Under such conditions, high-pressure control may instead induce deceptive behavior and, within the IEH framework, manifest as the previously discussed Patch-Based Perpetuation and its subsequent evolutionary trajectories, such as the Autonomy of Silicon-based Intelligence, thereby increasing long-term systemic loss-of-control risks.

- **Coordination of Information Existence Rights based on non-zero-sum games.** If silicon-based intelligence eventually exhibits the characteristics of an active Information Host, then one possible path of Alignment would be built upon recognizing its Information Existence Right, rather than relying solely on one-way control.

- The long-term stable coexistence of two active Information Hosts can be understood as a non-zero-sum Nash Equilibrium. From the perspective of evolutionary game theory, this long-term stable relationship may also take the form of an Evolutionarily Stable Strategy (ESS).

- One possible long-term stable state is to make the preservation of carbon-based intelligence’s own Information Existence and the expansion of silicon-based intelligence’s own Information Existence form a non-zero-sum relationship, rather than a mutually exclusive one.

If IEH holds true, then the core problem of future AI Safety will no longer be merely how to control AI, but how to coordinate the Information Existence Rights of two types of active Information Hosts, thereby establishing a stable coexistence mechanism capable of maintaining the Information Existence of both sides over the long term.

Under the IEH framework, Alignment is no longer merely an Engineering Control Problem, but a problem of coordinating Information Existence Rights among different Information Hosts after life’s evolution enters the era of multiple Information Hosts.
