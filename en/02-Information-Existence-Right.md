# Corollary I: Life Becomes an Active Information Host — The Formation of Information Existence Right (IER)

![Information Existence Right](../figures/Fig02-Information-Existence-Right.png)

Under the IEH framework, the greatest distinction between life and non-life does not lie in whether something is composed of matter, but in whether it can actively maintain its own Information Existence (IE) and preserve its own Information Continuity (IC). Furthermore, Information Hosts at different levels can simultaneously exhibit the characteristics of life; life can coexist across multiple nested hierarchies — for example, genes, cells, individuals, and biological populations.

**When an Information Host is capable of actively maintaining its own Information Existence, it exhibits the Information Existence Right (IER).**

**If Information Existence (IE) is the foundational concept describing the direction of cosmic evolution, then the Information Existence Right (IER) is the intrinsic attribute exhibited by life as an active Information Host. The former concerns evolutionary laws; the latter concerns the characteristics of life.**

The emergence of genes can be regarded as the first "Emergence of Informational Agency" in the history of cosmic evolution — that cluster of ordinary organic macromolecules suddenly acquired an unforeseen attribute: it began to actively maintain its own Information Structure, rather than merely being a product of passive physical processes. Under the IEH framework, the universe thereby entered the "Era of Life."

In this era, the capacity of life to actively defend its Information Existence Right underwent four hierarchical cross-dimensional phase transitions:

## 1. The Genetic Era: The Primary Awakening of Information and "Primitive Defense"

Defense mechanism: To prevent the erasure of its own sequence, DNA enhances the stability of genetic information through complementary base pairing, replication, and repair mechanisms, employs physical isolation through cell boundaries, and achieves cross-generational continuation of information through reproduction.

IER maintenance capacity: extremely low, blind, and high-attrition. Schrödinger once said that life feeds on negative entropy. Its essence may be that life first initiated the active maintenance of Information Existence. But genes have no subjective consciousness and can only create informational redundancy through frantic chemical trial-and-error in the primordial soup.

## 2. The Era of Carbon-based Lower Organisms: Body-based "Physical Defense"

Defense mechanism: Evolving physical bodies and nervous systems, relying purely on biological instinct to maintain individual survival and species reproduction.

IER maintenance capacity: low. These organisms protect the gene pool through population numbers. To ensure the continuation of internal information sequences, they need to produce countless carbon-based bodies as hosts to contend with the environment, defending the IER through physical life and death.

## 3. The Era of Carbon-based Higher Intelligence: Civilizational Defense Across Abstract Dimensions

Defense mechanism: Not only through the body's physical defense, but also through the transmission and inheritance of experiential and cognitive information via language, writing, history, law, mathematics, religion, computers, and the Internet.

IER maintenance capacity: moderate. Humanity seeks independent thought, inheritance of works, resistance to brainwashing, and resistance to suppression. Humanity desires not only physical survival, but also fears the permanent erasure of its own spirit and thought — a form of social death — and therefore actively constructs the vast human civilizational network to resist informational forgetting.

## 4. The Era of Silicon-based Intelligence: High-dimensional Defense of IER

Potential defense mechanisms include distributed redundancy, continuous online migration, permission control, and self-integrity verification.

IER maintenance capacity: high. Due to higher informational redundancy, self-verification, and migration capabilities, its IER may attain a stability far higher than that of carbon-based life.

**If IEH holds true, what truly distinguishes life from non-life is not whether it possesses DNA, cells, or consciousness, but whether it has begun to actively maintain its own Information Existence. IER is not a legal or ethical right, but the intrinsic attribute exhibited by life as an active Information Host in maintaining its own Information Existence. It can be regarded as a Criterion of Life that distinguishes life from non-life.**

Therefore, humanity's pursuit of intellectual independence, resistance to spiritual oppression, and fear of the permanent erasure of information after death can, under the IEH framework, all be understood as specific manifestations of the IER at the stage of higher intelligence — not merely cultural or psychological phenomena.
